# Council Decision (EU) 2026/2536 of 26 October 2026 on the position to be taken on behalf of the European Union within the Council of Members of the International Olive Council concerning the extension of the date for notifying acceptance of the amendment to Article 36 of the International Agreement on Olive Oil and Table Olives, 2015

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 207(4), first subparagraph, in conjunction with Article 218(9), thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the European Commission,

Whereas:

- (1) The International Agreement on Olive Oil and Table Olives, 2015 [^ntr1-L_202602536EN.000101-E0001] (the ‘Agreement’) was concluded by the Union by means of Council Decision (EU) 2019/848 [^ntr2-L_202602536EN.000101-E0002]. The Agreement remains in force until 31 December 2026 pursuant to Article 36(1) thereof.

- (2) It is possible for the Council of Members of the International Olive Council (the ‘Council of Members’) to amend the Agreement pursuant to Article 32(1) thereof. The Council of Members, at its 119th session in June 2024, amended Article 36 of the Agreement by consensus in order to allow the extension of the Agreement for successive periods not exceeding five years on each occasion (the ‘amendment’). The amendment was approved on behalf of the Union by means of Council Decision (EU) 2026/1368 [^ntr3-L_202602536EN.000101-E0003]. The Council of Members decided that Members of the International Olive Council (the ‘Members’) were to notify the depositary of their acceptance of the amendment by 24 June 2026. The amendment was intended to enter into force on 1 January 2027 on the condition that all the Members made such a notification.

- (3) At its 123rd session in June 2026, the Council of Members proposed that the date by which Members were to notify the depositary of their acceptance of the amendment be extended to 24 June 2027.

- (4) It is therefore appropriate to establish the position to be taken within the Council of Members with regard to the extension of the date by which Members are to notify the depositary of their acceptance of the amendment,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The position to be taken on the Union’s behalf within the Council of Members of the International Olive Council during its future sessions or in the framework of a procedure for the adoption of decisions by the Council of Members by an exchange of correspondence shall be to agree to extend to 24 June 2027 the date by which Members are to notify the depositary of their acceptance of the amendment to Article 36 of the International Agreement on Olive Oil and Table Olives, 2015.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 26 October 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**M. HEYDON**
