# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2025/2219 of 3 November 2025 imposing provisional anti-dumping duties on imports of softwood plywood originating in the Federative Republic of Brazil

#### Article 1

The rates of the provisional anti-dumping duty applicable to the net, free-at-Union-frontier price, before duty, of the product described in paragraph 1 and produced by the companies listed below shall be as follows:

| Country of origin | Company | Provisional anti-dumping duty (%) | TARIC additional code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brazil | Indústria de Compensados Sudati Ltda. Conply Indústria de Compensados Ltda. Indústria de Compensados Guararapes Ltda. | 5,4 | 89XQ |
|  | Other cooperating companies listed in Annex | 5,4 | See Annex |
|  | All other imports originating in country concerned | 5,4 | 8999 |

Member States shall, on a monthly basis, inform the Commission of the number of m3 imported under CN code 4412 39 00 .
