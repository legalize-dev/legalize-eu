| 16.2.2007 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 46/14 |

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 of 20 March 2006 on the protection of geographical indications and designations of origin for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0001], and in particular the first subparagraph of Article 7(4) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) In accordance with the first subparagraph of Article 6(2) and pursuant to Article 17(2) of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006, Member States’ applications to register certain names were published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* as follows:

- — as regards Belgium: ‘Geraardsbergse mattentaart’
  [^ntr2-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0002];

- — as regards Spain: ‘Pataca de Galicia’ or ‘Patata de Galicia’
  [^ntr3-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0003], ‘Poniente de Granada’
  [^ntr4-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0004], ‘Gata-Hurdes’
  [^ntr5-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0005], ‘Patatas de Prades’ or ‘Patates de Prades’
  [^ntr6-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0006] and ‘Mantequilla de Soria’
  [^ntr7-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0007];

- — as regards France: ‘Huile d'olive de Nîmes’
  [^ntr8-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0008], ‘Huile d'olive de Corse’ or ‘Huile d'olive de Corse-Oliu di Corsica’
  [^ntr9-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0009], ‘Clémentine de Corse’
  [^ntr10-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0010] and ‘Agneau de Sisteron’
  [^ntr11-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0011];

- — as regards Ireland: ‘Connemara Hill Lamb’ or ‘Uain Sléibhe Chonamara’
  [^ntr12-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0012];

- — as regards Italy: ‘Sardegna’
  [^ntr13-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0013], ‘Carota dell'Altopiano del Fucino’
  [^ntr14-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0014], ‘Stelvio’ or ‘Stilfser’
  [^ntr15-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0015] and ‘Limone Femminello del Gargano’
  [^ntr16-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0016];

- — as regards Portugal: ‘Azeitonas de Conserva de Elvas e Campo Maior’
  [^ntr17-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0017], ‘Chouriça de Carne de Barroso-Montalegre’
  [^ntr18-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0018], ‘Chouriço de Abóbora de Barroso-Montalegre’
  [^ntr19-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0019], ‘Sangueira de Barroso-Montalegre’
  [^ntr20-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0020], ‘Batata de Trás-os-Montes’
  [^ntr21-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0021], ‘Salpicão de Barroso-Montalegre’
  [^ntr22-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0022], ‘Alheira de Barroso-Montalegre’
  [^ntr23-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0023], ‘Cordeiro de Barroso’ or ‘Anho de Barroso’ or ‘Borrego de leite de Barroso’
  [^ntr24-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0024], ‘Azeite do Alentejo Interior’
  [^ntr25-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0025], ‘Paio de Beja’
  [^ntr26-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0026] and ‘Linguíça do Baixo Alentejo’ or ‘Chouriço de carne do Baixo Alentejo’
  [^ntr27-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0027];

- — as regards Slovenia: ‘Ekstra deviško oljčno olje Slovenske Istre’
  [^ntr28-L_2007046EN.01001401-E0028].

- (2) As no objection under Article 7 of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 was sent to the Commission, these names should be entered in the ‘Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications’,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The names in the Annex to this Regulation are hereby entered in the ‘Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications’.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 15 February 2007.

***For the Commission***

**Mariann FISCHER BOEL**

***Member of the Commission***
