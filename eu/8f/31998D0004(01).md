# Decision of the European Central Bank of 9 June 1998 on the adoption of the Conditions of Employment for Staff of the European Central Bank as amended on 31 March 1999 (ECB/1998/4)

#### Article 1

The Conditions of Employment for Staff of the ECB are hereby adopted.

#### Article 2

For information to all interested parties, the conditions of employment of Staff of the ECB shall be accessible to the public on the website of the ECB (http://www.ecb.int).

#### Article 3

This Decision shall be published in the *Official Journal of the European Communities*.
