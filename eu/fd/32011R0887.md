# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 887/2011 of 5 September 2011 concerning the authorisation of a preparation of Enterococcus faecium CECT 4515 as feed additive for chickens for fattening (holder of the authorisation Norel SA)  Text with EEA relevance

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) No 887/2011

of 5 September 2011

concerning the authorisation of a preparation of *Enterococcus faecium* CECT 4515 as feed additive for chickens for fattening (holder of the authorisation  Evonik Nutrition & Care GmbH)

(Text with EEA relevance)

#### Article 1

The preparation specified in the Annex, belonging to the additive category ‘zootechnical additives’ and to the functional group ‘gut flora stabilisers’, is authorised as an additive in animal nutrition subject to the conditions laid down in that Annex.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
