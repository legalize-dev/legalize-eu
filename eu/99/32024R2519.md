THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU) No 36/2012 of 18 January 2012 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Syria and repealing Regulation (EU) No 442/2011 [^ntr1-L_202402519EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 32 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

- (1) On 18 January 2012, the Council adopted Regulation (EU) No 36/2012.

- (2) Following the judgment of the General Court in Case T-209/22 [^ntr2-L_202402519EN.000101-E0002], one entry should be deleted from the list of natural and legal persons, entities or bodies set out in Annex II to Regulation (EU) No 36/2012.

- (3) Regulation (EU) No 36/2012 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Annex II to Regulation (EU) No 36/2012 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 23 September 2024.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**Z. FELDMAN**

[^ntr1-L_202402519EN.000101-E0001]: [OJ L 16, 19.1.2012, p. 1](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/JOL_2012_016_R_TOC).

[^ntr2-L_202402519EN.000101-E0002]: Judgment of the General Court (Ninth Chamber, Extended Composition) of 17 July 2024, *Shahla Makhlouf* v *Council of the European Union*, T-209/22, ECLI:EU:T:2024:498.

## ANNEX

The following entry is deleted from the list set out in Section A (‘Persons’) of Annex II to Regulation (EU) No 36/2012:

> - ‘320. Shalaa Mohammed MAKHLOUF’.
