THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU) No 904/2010 of 7 October 2010 on administrative cooperation and combating fraud in the field of value added tax [^ntr1-L_202602210EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 49(3) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Annex IV to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 79/2012 [^ntr2-L_202602210EN.000101-E0002] sets out the list of statistical data that the Member States are to communicate to the Commission for the evaluation of Regulation (EU) No 904/2010.

- (2) Regulation (EU) No 904/2010, as amended by Council Regulation (EU) 2020/283 [^ntr3-L_202602210EN.000101-E0003], laid down the Central Electronic System of Payment information (CESOP), which is a new electronic system for administrative cooperation and fight against fraud in the field of VAT. It is therefore necessary to amend Implementing Regulation (EU) No 79/2012 as regards the statistical data needed to evaluate the functioning of CESOP.

- (3) Furthermore, Council Directive (EU) 2020/285 [^ntr4-L_202602210EN.000101-E0004] amended Council Directive 2006/112/EC [^ntr5-L_202602210EN.000101-E0005] on the common system of value added tax as regards the special scheme for small enterprises and Regulation (EU) No 904/2010 for the purpose of monitoring the correct application of the special VAT scheme for small enterprises. To allow for the effective monitoring of the functioning of the special scheme for small enterprises, Annex IV to Implementing Regulation (EU) No 79/2012 should also be amended to include statistical data in relation to the special scheme for small enterprises.

- (4) Implementing Regulation (EU) No 79/2012 should therefore be amended accordingly.

- (5) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Administrative Cooperation,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Annex IV to Implementing Regulation (EU) No 79/2012 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 5 October 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
