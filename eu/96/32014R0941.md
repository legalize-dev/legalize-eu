# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 941/2014 of 3 September 2014 entering a name in the register of traditional specialities guaranteed [Роле Трапезица (Role Trapezitsa) (TSG)]

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4.9.2014 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 264/1 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 November 2012 on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_2014264EN.01000101-E0001], and in particular Article 52(2) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Pursuant to Article 50(2)(b) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012, Bulgaria's application to register the name ‘Роле Трапезица’ (Role Trapezitsa) was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr2-L_2014264EN.01000101-E0002].

- (2) As no statement of opposition under Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 has been received by the Commission, the name ‘Роле Трапезица’ (Role Trapezitsa) should therefore be entered in the register,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name ‘Роле Трапезица’ (Role Trapezitsa) (TSG) is hereby entered in the register.

The name referred to in the first paragraph identifies a product in Class 1.2. Meat products (cooked, salted, smoked, etc.) set out in Annex XI to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 668/2014 [^ntr3-L_2014264EN.01000101-E0003].

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 3 September 2014.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**José Manuel BARROSO**
