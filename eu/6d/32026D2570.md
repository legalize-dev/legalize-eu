# Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2570 of 16 November 2026 amending Decision (CFSP) 2024/385 establishing restrictive measures against Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad and those who support, facilitate or enable their violent actions

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 29 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

- (1) On 19 January 2024, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2024/385 [^ntr1-L_202602570EN.000101-E0001].

- (2) In view of the continued threat posed by Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad, one person and one entity should be added to the list of natural or legal persons, groups, entities and bodies set out in the Annex to Decision (CFSP) 2024/385.

- (3) It is also appropriate to clarify that Member States may grant exemptions from the travel ban provided for in Decision (CFSP) 2024/385 where entry or transit is necessary for the fulfilment of a judicial process or for the serving by a listed person of a custodial sentence in a Member State.

- (4) Decision (CFSP) 2024/385 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

Decision (CFSP) 2024/385 is amended as follows:

- (1) Article 1(7) is replaced by the following:

> ‘Member States may also grant exemptions from the measures imposed under paragraph 1 where entry or transit is necessary for the fulfilment of a judicial process or for the serving by a person listed in the Annex of a custodial sentence in a Member State.’

;

- (2) the Annex is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Decision.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

Done at Brussels, 16 November 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**K. KALLAS**
