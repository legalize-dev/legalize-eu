# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/219 of 3 January 2024 conferring protection under Article 99 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the name Terre Abruzzesi / Terre d’Abruzzo (PGI)

# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2024/219 of 3 January 2024 conferring protection under Article 99 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the name ‘Terre Abruzzesi / Terre d’Abruzzo’ (PGI)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007 (1), and in particular Article 99(1) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) In accordance with Article 97(2) and (4) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013, the Commission has examined the application to register the name ‘Terre Abruzzesi / Terre d’Abruzzo’ forwarded by Italy and has published it in the Official Journal of the European Union (2).

(2) No statement of objection has been received by the Commission under Article 98 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013.

(3) In accordance with Article 99(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013, the name ‘Terre Abruzzesi / Terre d’Abruzzo’ should be protected and entered in the register referred to in Article 104 of that Regulation,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name ‘Terre Abruzzesi / Terre d’Abruzzo’ (PGI) is hereby protected.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 3 January 2024.

**For the Commission,
 On behalf of the President,
 Janusz WOJCIECHOWSKI
 Member of the Commission**
