# Commission Regulation (EC) No 1070/2008 of 30 October 2008 entering a name in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications (Rogal świętomarciński (PGI))

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 31.10.2008 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 290/16 |

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 of 20 March 2006 on the protection of geographical indications and designations of origin for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_2008290EN.01001601-E0001], and in particular the first subparagraph of Article 7(4) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 6(2) of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006, and in accordance with Article 17(2) thereof, Poland’s application to register the name ‘Rogal świętomarciński’ was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr2-L_2008290EN.01001601-E0002].

- (2) As no objection within the meaning of Article 7 of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 has been received by the Commission, this name should be entered in the register,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name contained in the Annex to this Regulation is hereby entered in the register.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 30 October 2008.

***For the Commission***

**Mariann FISCHER BOEL**

***Member of the Commission***

Foodstuffs listed in Annex I to the Regulation:

### Class 2.4.   Bread, pastry, cakes, confectionery, biscuits and other baker’s wares

### POLAND

Rogal świętomarciński (PGI)
