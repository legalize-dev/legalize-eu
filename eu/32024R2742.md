# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2742 of 18 October 2024 registering a name in the register of protected designation of origin and protected geographical indications (Urbezo (PDO))

# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2024/2742 of 18 October 2024 registering a name in the register of protected designation of origin and protected geographical indications (‘Urbezo’ (PDO))

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007 (1), and in particular Article 99 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 of the European Parliament and of the Council (2) deleted, among others, Articles 96 to 99 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013. In accordance with Article 90(1) and (2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 those Articles remain applicable to the applications for registration of geographical indications received by the Commission and published in the Official Journal of the European Union before 13 May 2024.

(2) In accordance with Article 97(2) and (3) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013, as applicable before the entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2021/2117 of the European Parliament and of the Council (3), the Commission has examined the application to register the name ‘Urbezo’ as protected designation of origin forwarded by Spain and has published it in the Official Journal of the European Union (4).

(3) No statement of objection has been received by the Commission under Article 98 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013.

(4) In accordance with Article 99 of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013, as applicable pursuant Article 5(1) of Regulation (EU) 2021/2117, the examination procedure referred to in Article 229(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 applies to decisions concerning applications for registrations received by the Commission before 7 December 2021.

(5) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee for the Common Organisation of the Agricultural Markets,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name ‘Urbezo’ (PDO) is hereby registered.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 18 October 2024.

**For the Commission,
 On behalf of the President,
 Janusz WOJCIECHOWSKI
 Member of the Commission**
