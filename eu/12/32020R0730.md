# Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/730 of 3 June 2020 implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/1509 concerning restrictive measures against the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea

COUNCIL IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2020/730

of 2 June 2020

implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/1509 concerning restrictive measures against the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea

#### Article 1

Annex XIII to Regulation (EU) 2017/1509 is amended as set out in the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

## ANNEX

In Regulation (EU) 2017/1509, Annex XIII, Part (b) (‘Legal persons, entities and bodies’), entry 75 is replaced by the following:
