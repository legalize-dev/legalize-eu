# Commission Regulation (EC) No 1443/2006 of 29 September 2006 concerning the permanent authorisations of certain additives in feedingstuffs and an authorisation for 10 years for a coccidiostat   (Text with EEA relevance)

#### Article 2

The preparation belonging to the group ‘Coccidiostat and other medicinal substances’, as specified in Annex II, is authorised for 10 years as additive in animal nutrition under the conditions laid down in that Annex.

#### Article 4

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
