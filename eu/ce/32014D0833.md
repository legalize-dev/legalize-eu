# 2014/833/EU: Commission Implementing Decision of 25 November 2014 concerning certain protective measures in relation to recent outbreaks of highly pathogenic avian influenza of subtype H5N8 in the Netherlands (notified under document C(2014) 9126)  Text with EEA relevance

#### Article 1

The Netherlands shall ensure that the protection and surveillance zones established in accordance with Article 16(1) of Directive 2005/94/EC comprise at least the areas listed as protection and surveillance zones in Parts A and B of the Annex to this Decision.

#### Article 2

Implementing Decision 2014/808/EU is repealed.

#### Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the Kingdom of the Netherlands.
