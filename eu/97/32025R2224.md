# Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/2224 of 5 November 2025 amending Annex II to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council and Annex II to Directive 2002/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards monosodium salt of L-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid as a source of folic acid added to foods and as a source of folate used in the manufacture of food supplements

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Directive 2002/46/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 10 June 2002 on the approximation of the laws of the Member States relating to food supplements [^ntr1-L_202502224EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 4(5) thereof,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006 on the addition of vitamins and minerals and of certain other substances to foods [^ntr2-L_202502224EN.000101-E0002], and in particular Article 3(3) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Annexes I and II to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 establish the list of vitamin and mineral substances which may be added to foods and the forms in which they may be added.

- (2) Annexes I and II to Directive 2002/46/EC establish the list of vitamin and mineral substances which may be used in the manufacture of food supplements and the forms in which they may be added.

- (3) On 26 October 2023, the European Food Safety Authority (‘the Authority’) issued a scientific opinion [^ntr3-L_202502224EN.000101-E0003] in the framework of the procedure for authorisation of a novel food pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2015/2283 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr4-L_202502224EN.000101-E0004]. In the mandate, the Authority was asked to deliver an opinion on the safety of monosodium salt of l-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid (5-MTHF) as a novel food and to address the bioavailability of folate from this source in the context of Directive 2002/46/EC, Regulation (EU) No 609/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr5-L_202502224EN.000101-E0005] and Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006. The Authority in the opinion concluded that monosodium salt of L-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid is safe under the proposed conditions of use and that it is a source from which folate is bioavailable.

- (4) Based on the favourable opinion of the Authority, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1037 [^ntr6-L_202502224EN.000101-E0006] authorised, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2015/2283, the placing on the market of monosodium salt of L-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid as a novel food for use in certain foods and food supplements, subject to specific conditions.

- (5) The Commission considers that the Authority’s opinion gives sufficient grounds to establish that the use of monosodium salt of L-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid as authorised by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1037, is not of safety concern as a source of folic acid when added to foods and as a source of folate when used in the manufacture of food supplements.

- (6) Based on the Authority’s favourable opinion and on the authorisation as a novel food, monosodium salt of L-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid should be included in Annex II to Directive 2002/46/EC as a form of folate and in Annex II to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 as a form of folic acid.

- (7) The Advisory Group on the Food Chain and Animal and Plant Health was consulted, and its comments were taken into consideration.

- (8) Annex II to Directive 2002/46/EC and Annex II to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 should therefore be amended accordingly.

- (9) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Plants, Animals, Food and Feed,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Annex II to Directive 2002/46/EC is amended in accordance with Annex I to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

Annex II to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006 is amended in accordance with Annex II to this Regulation.

#### Article 3

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 5 November 2025.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

## ANNEX I

## Amendment to Directive 2002/46/EC

In point A.10 (FOLATE) of Annex II to Directive 2002/46/EC, the following entry is inserted after the entry for ‘(6S)-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid, glucosamine salt’:

> - ‘(d) monosodium salt of L-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid[^ntr*1-L_202502224EN.000301-E0001]

> [^ntr*1-L_202502224EN.000301-E0001]: As listed in the Union list of novel foods in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/2470 of 20 December 2017 establishing the Union list of novel foods in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2015/2283 of the European Parliament and of the Council on novel foods ([OJ L 351, 30.12.2017, p. 72](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/JOL_2017_351_R_TOC), ELI: [http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2017/2470/oj](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2017/2470/oj)).’.’

## ANNEX II

## Amendment to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006

In point 1 (Vitamin formulations) of Annex II to Regulation (EC) No 1925/2006, under the title FOLIC ACID, the following entry is inserted after the entry for ‘calcium-L-methylfolate’:

> ‘monosodium salt of L-5-methyltetrahydrofolic acid[^ntr*1-L_202502224EN.000401-E0001]

> [^ntr*1-L_202502224EN.000401-E0001]: As listed in the Union list of novel foods in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/2470 of 20 December 2017 establishing the Union list of novel foods in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2015/2283 of the European Parliament and of the Council on novel foods ([OJ L 351, 30.12.2017, p. 72](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/JOL_2017_351_R_TOC), ELI: [http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2017/2470/oj)](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2017/2470/oj)).’.’
