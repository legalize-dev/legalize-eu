# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/143 of 2 February 2022 on cancelling the registration of protected geographical indication ‘Volailles de Loué’ (PGI)

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3.2.2022 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 24/1 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 November 2012 on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_2022024EN.01000101-E0001], and in particular Article 54(1) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Article 7(1) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 664/2014 [^ntr2-L_2022024EN.01000101-E0002] provides that the procedure laid down in Articles 49 to 52 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 applies mutatis mutandis to the cancellation of a registration as referred to in Article 54(1) of that Regulation.

- (2) Pursuant to Article 50(2)(a) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012, in conjunction with Article 7(1) of Delegated Regulation (EU) No 664/2014, application sent by France to cancel the registration of the protected geographical indication (PGI) ‘Volailles de Loué’ was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr3-L_2022024EN.01000101-E0003].

- (3) As no statement of opposition under Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 has been received by the Commission, the name ‘Volailles de Loué’ (PGI) should therefore be cancelled from the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications.

- (4) Pursuant to the last subparagraph of Article 54(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 such cancellation is adopted in accordance with the examination procedure referred to in Article 57(2) of that Regulation.

- (5) The measure provided for in this Regulation is in accordance with the opinion of the Agricultural Product Quality Policy Committee,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The registration of the name ‘Volailles de Loué’ (PGI) is hereby cancelled.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 2 February 2022.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
