THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 28(1) and Article 41(2) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

- (1) On 24 March 2025, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2025/609 [^ntr1-L_202602375EN.000101-E0001].

- (2) On 28 May 2026, the Political and Security Committee supported the intended reallocation of available funds in 2026, including an increase of EUR 8 000 000 for the assistance measure to support the Armed Forces of Mauritania.

- (3) Complementary support, including technical naval assistance, needs to be provided for a period beyond the current end date of the implementation period of Decision (CFSP) 2025/609. Therefore, the duration of the assistance measures should be extended by 24 months from 24 March 2028 to 23 March 2030.

- (4) Decision (CFSP) 2025/609 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

Decision (CFSP) 2025/609 is amended as follows:

- (1) in Article 1, paragraph 4 is replaced by the following:

> ‘4.   The duration of the assistance measure shall be 60 months from the date of adoption of this Decision.’

;

- (2) in Article 2, paragraph 1 is replaced by the following:

> ‘1.   The financial reference amount intended to cover the expenditure related to the assistance measure shall be EUR 28 000 000.’.

#### Article 2

##### Entry into force

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 26 October 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**M. HEYDON**
