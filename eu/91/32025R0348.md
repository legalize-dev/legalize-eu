THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 314/2004 of 19 February 2004 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Zimbabwe [^ntr1-L_202500348EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 11(b) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) In Articles 4 and 5 of Council Decision 2011/101/CFSP [^ntr2-L_202500348EN.000101-E0002] the persons and entities to whom restrictive measures apply are identified.

- (2) Regulation (EC) No 314/2004 gives effect to Decision 2011/101/CFSP to the extent that action at Union level is required. In particular, Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 314/2004 lists the persons and entities covered by the freezing of funds and economic resources under that Regulation.

- (3) On 18 February 2025, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2025/335 [^ntr3-L_202500348EN.000101-E0003] removing the last entity from the list of persons and entities subject to restrictive measures.

- (4) Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 314/2004 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 314/2004 is amended as set out in the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all the Member States.

Done at Brussels, 18 February 2025.

***For the Commission***

***On behalf of The President***

***Director-General***

***Directorate-General for Financial Stability, Financial Services and Capital Markets Union***
