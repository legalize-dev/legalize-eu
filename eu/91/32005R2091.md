# Commission Regulation (EC) No 2091/2005 of  15 December 2005  publishing, for 2006, the agricultural product nomenclature for export refunds introduced by Regulation (EEC) No 3846/87

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24.12.2005 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 343/1 |

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 1784/2003 of 29 September 2003 on the common organisation of the market in cereals [^ntr1-L_2005343EN.01000101-E0001], and the other regulations on the common organisation of the markets in agricultural products,

Having regard to Commission Regulation (EEC) No 3846/87 of 17 December 1987 establishing an agricultural product nomenclature for export refunds [^ntr2-L_2005343EN.01000101-E0002], and in particular the fourth paragraph of Article 3,

Whereas:

- (1) The full version of the refund nomenclature valid at 1 January 2006, as it ensues from the regulatory provisions on export arrangements for agricultural products, should be published.

- (2) The amendments to the Combined Nomenclature applicable from 2006 concerning cheeses introduced by Regulation (EC) No 1719/2005 [^ntr3-L_2005343EN.01000101-E0003] must be taken into consideration and the refund nomenclature must be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Regulation (EEC) No 3846/87 is amended as follows:

- 1. Annex I is replaced by Annex I to this Regulation;

- 2. Annex II is replaced by Annex II to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on 1 January 2006.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 15 December 2005.

***For the Commission***

**Mariann FISCHER BOEL**

***Member of the Commission***
