THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 April 2024 establishing a framework for ensuring a secure and sustainable supply of critical raw materials and amending Regulations (EU) No 168/2013, (EU) 2018/858, (EU) 2018/1724 and (EU) 2019/1020 [^ntr1-L_202602349EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 7(9) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Pursuant to Article 1(2), point (a) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 and in view of securing the Union’s supply of critical raw materials, the Commission is to identify projects for the extraction, processing, recycling or substitution of strategic raw materials, with the aim of recognising such projects as Strategic Projects under the framework laid down in Article 7 of that Regulation.

- (2) In accordance with Article 7(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, the Commission launched on 23 May 2024 a call to apply for the recognition of a critical raw material project as a Strategic Project [^ntr2-L_202602349EN.000101-E0002], with a second cut-off date on 15 January 2026.

- (3) Following the submission of applications, the Commission informed the applicants on 13 February 2026 whether the information provided was considered complete in accordance with Article 7(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252.

- (4) At the time of the second cut-off date on 15 January 2026, 101 applications were submitted from proposed projects located in Member States, 98 of which the Commission considered complete in accordance with Article 7(1) and (4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252.

- (5) In accordance with Article 7(5) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, the Commission informed the European Critical Raw Materials Board (‘the Board’) established by Article 35(1) of that Regulation of the applications the Commission considered complete.

- (6) Thereafter, the Commission transmitted the full applications, in accordance with Article 7(7) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, to Member States whose territory is concerned by the proposed project or projects. The Member States concerned had the opportunity to provide technical feedback and object to those proposed projects in accordance with Article 7(8) of that Regulation.

- (7) The Commission assessed those applications in accordance with the criteria specified in Article 6(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, with the support of external experts with professional expertise in the technical, financial, environmental, social and governance (ESG) dimensions of a project, as well as in the United Nations Framework Classification for Resources (UNFC) [^ntr3-L_202602349EN.000101-E0003]. The expert report provided the basis for the Commission’s assessment.

- (8) In particular, the Commission assessed those applications in light of their contribution to the security of the Union’s supply of strategic raw materials, technical feasibility, sustainable implementation and cross-border benefits, pursuant to Article 6(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. The projects that fulfil all the criteria set out in that Article should therefore be recognised as Strategic Projects.

- (9) The Commission provided the Board with its assessment in accordance with Article 7(6), second subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. None of the projects that fulfil all the criteria provided for in Article 6(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 listed in the Annex to this Decision received an objection from the Member States whose territory is concerned by the proposed projects in accordance with Article 7(8) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252.

- (10) The Commission should therefore notify all applicants for the recognition of a project as Strategic Project. In particular, the Commission should notify all applicants of the result of its assessment and the reasoning thereof ensuring the protection of trade and business secrets and other sensitive, confidential and classified information, in accordance with Article 46 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252.

- (11) The Commission is to provide this Decision to the Board and to the Member State or Member States whose territory is concerned by the project or projects, in accordance with Article 7(9), second subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252.

- (12) This Decision is in accordance with the opinion of the Board,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The projects listed in the Annex are recognised as Strategic Projects.

## ANNEX

## LIST OF STRATEGIC PROJECTS

(1)

3ECYCLE is an ongoing recycling project in the Netherlands. The project promoted by Mitsubishi Materials Europe B.V aims to contribute to the supply of copper.

(2)

9PV-up: Upcycling raw materials from end-of-life photovoltaic panels. is an ongoing recycling project in Italy. The project promoted by 9-Tech aims to contribute to the supply of bauxite/alumina/aluminium, copper and silicon metal.

(3)

ALE – Altmark Lithium Extraction is an ongoing integrated project in Germany. The project promoted by Neptune Energy Innovations GmbH aims to contribute to the supply of lithium (battery grade).

(4)

Alkeemia PureGraph is an ongoing processing project in Italy. The project promoted by Alkeemia S.p.A. aims to contribute to the supply of graphite (battery grade).

(5)

EU APT Tungsten Plant is an ongoing processing project in Spain. The project promoted by ABENÓJAR TUNGSTEN S.A. aims to contribute to the supply of tungsten.

(6)

BORRALHA MINING PROJECT is an ongoing extraction project in Portugal. The project promoted by MINERALIA – Minas, Geotecnia e Construções, Lda. aims to contribute to the supply of tungsten.

(7)

BREES (Best Rare Earth Element Separation) is an ongoing recycling project in France. The project promoted by Rhodia Operations aims to contribute to the supply of rare earth elements for magnets.

(8)

CRM4Defence – Byproducts and Recycling of CRM for Defence is an ongoing recycling project in Italy, Poland, United Kingdom and France. The project promoted by Leonardo S.p.A. aims to contribute to the supply of bauxite/alumina/aluminium, magnesium and titanium-metal.

(9)

Cobre San Rafael Project is an ongoing extraction project in Spain. The project promoted by Cobre San Rafael, S.L. aims to contribute to the supply of copper.

(10)

CoNiKo – Cobalt and Nickel refining in Kokkola is an ongoing processing project in Finland. The project promoted by Umicore aims to contribute to the supply of cobalt and nickel (battery grade).

(11)

CUPIDON is an ongoing recycling project in Belgium. The project promoted by Comet Traitements aims to contribute to the supply of copper.

(12)

Development and extraction of the Retków-Grodziszcze Deposit in KGHM Polska Miedź S.A. is an ongoing extraction project in Poland. The project promoted by KGHM Polska Miedź S.A. aims to contribute to the supply of copper.

(13)

EMME is an ongoing integrated project in France. The project promoted by ELECTRO MOBILITY MATERIALS EUROPE (EMME) aims to contribute to the supply of cobalt and nickel (battery grade).

(14)

Establishment of electric battery recycling capacity, Racari city, Dambovita county is an ongoing recycling project in Romania. The project promoted by ECO LIFE STYLE S.R.L. aims to contribute to the supply of cobalt, lithium (battery grade), manganese (battery grade), graphite (battery grade) and nickel (battery grade).

(15)

ETEOCLES – Developing a recycling hub for low quality copper scrap is an ongoing recycling project in Greece. The project promoted by ElvalHalcor Hellenic Copper and Aluminium industry S.A. aims to contribute to the supply of copper.

(16)

EU BEST STRAT: Battery Economical Sustainable (Recycling-) Technology is an ongoing recycling project in Germany.
