THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/631 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 setting CO2 emission performance standards for new passenger cars and for new light commercial vehicles, and repealing Regulations (EC) No 443/2009 and (EU) No 510/2011 [^ntr1-L_202401294EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 13(4), second subparagraph, thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) The Dutch and German language versions of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2867 [^ntr2-L_202401294EN.000101-E0002] contain an error in Article 1(2), point (b), as regards the circumstances under which that regulation would not apply to certain manufacturers. The error affects the substance of the act.

- (2) The Dutch and German language versions of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2867 should therefore be corrected accordingly. The other language versions are not affected,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

*(Does not concern the English language.)*

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 1 March 2024.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
