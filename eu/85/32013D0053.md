# 2013/53/EU: Council Implementing Decision of 22 January 2013 authorising the Kingdom of Belgium to introduce a special measure derogating from Article 285 of Directive 2006/112/EC on the common system of value added tax

#### Article 1

By way of derogation from Article 285 of Directive 2006/112/EC, the Kingdom of Belgium is authorised to exempt from VAT taxable persons whose annual turnover is no higher than EUR 25 000 .

The Kingdom of Belgium may raise that ceiling in order to maintain the value of the exemption in real terms.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall apply from 1 January 2013 until 31 December 2024.

#### Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the Kingdom of Belgium.
