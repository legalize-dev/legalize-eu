# Decision (EU) 2021/2255 of the European Central Bank of 7 December 2021 on the approval of the volume of coin issuance in 2022 (ECB/2021/54)

DECISION (EU) 2021/2255 OF THE EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK

of 7 December 2021

on the approval of the volume of coin issuance in 2022 (ECB/2021/54)

#### Article 1

##### Definitions

For the purposes of this Decision, the definitions in Article 1 of Decision (EU) 2015/2332 (ECB/2015/43) apply.

#### Article 3

##### Taking effect

This Decision shall take effect on the day of its notification to the addressees.

#### Article 4

##### Addressees

This Decision is addressed to the Member States whose currency is the euro.
