# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2407 of 29 October 2026 appointing the chairperson, members, alternates and observers of the Network Management Board and the members and alternates of the European Aviation Crisis Coordination Cell for 2027 and 2028

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Image omitted; see official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32026D2407) | Official Journal of the European Union | EN L series |

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | 2026/2407 | 30.10.2026 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/2803 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2024 on the Implementation of the Single European Sky [^ntr1-L_202602407EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Articles 37(5) and 38(15) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) The Network Management Board (NMB) and the European Aviation Crisis Coordination Cell (EACCC) are the governing bodies of the European Air Traffic Management Network (EATMN). The NMB is responsible to ensure appropriate governance over the execution of the network functions and the EACCC is responsible to ensure effective crisis management at EATMN level.

- (2) The Commission is mandated by Articles 18(7) and 19(2) of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 [^ntr2-L_202602407EN.000101-E0002] to appoint members and their alternates and observers in the NMB and members and their alternates in the EACCC respectively following the proposals as specified in those Articles.

- (3) The Commission is empowered to adopt implementing acts pursuant to Article 38(13) and (15) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2803 that could amend Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 as regards the composition of the NMB and of the EACCC compared to the current composition pursuant to Articles 18 and 19 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123, or repeal that Implementing Regulation. If those new implementing acts become applicable before 31 December 2028, this could affect the current composition of the NMB or EACCC.

- (4) As the current period of appointment of the members, their alternates and observers of the Network Management Board, and of the members and their alternates of the European Aviation Crisis Coordination Cell under Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/3136 [^ntr3-L_202602407EN.000101-E0003] expires on 31 December 2026 and in order to ensure continuity of the execution of the tasks of NMB and of EACCC, the Commission should appoint the members and their alternates and observers in the NMB and in the EACCC for a new period until 31 December 2028.

- (5) The persons appointed by this Decision were proposed to the Commission in line with Articles 18 and 19 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 and following an open call to the operational stakeholders, the Network Manager and Eurocontrol that are members and observers in the NMB and members in the EACCC. The objective of the call was to inform the Commission of any proposals for the members, alternates and observers in the NMB and members and their alternates in the EACCC for the period 2027-2028.

- (6) The represented organisations proposed to keep their current members, alternates and observers except for the cases described in recitals 8 to 14.

- (7) In these circumstances, the chairperson and the vice chairpersons of the NMB appointed through Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/3136 should be reappointed.

- (8) On 28 April 2026, the European Free Trade Association Surveillance Authority proposed to the Commission a change in their representative in the NMB.

- (9) Regarding the representation of air navigation service providers in the NMB, their organisations proposed to the Commission pursuant to Article 18(7) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 the following changes to their representation in line with Article 18(3)(a) of that Regulation. On 15 June 2026, DANUBE FAB proposed a change in their alternate member in the NMB. On 30 June 2026, FAB CE proposed a change in their alternate member in the NMB and BLUEMED FAB proposed a change in their member and alternate member in the NMB. On 2 July 2026, NEFAB proposed a change in their member and alternate member in the NMB.

- (10) Regarding the representation of two air navigation service providers of associated countries contributing to the work of the Network Manager in the NMB, on 2 July 2026, Eurocontrol proposed to the Commission pursuant to Article 18(7) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 a change in their representation in line with Article 18(4)(e) of that Regulation and with the requirement of Article 18(7) of that Regulation concerning appointment of the non-voting members based both on a rotation, which allows each associated country to be appointed in turn, and on the operational needs at the time of the appointment.

- (11) Regarding the representation of airspace users, the European Business Aviation Association (EBAA) proposed to the Commission on 30 June 2026 pursuant to Article 18(7) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 a change in their alternate member in the NMB in line with Article 18(3)(b) of that Regulation. Airlines for Europe (A4E) proposed to the Commission on 2 July pursuant to Article 18(7) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 a change in their alternate member in the NMB in line with Article 18(3)(b) of that Regulation. A4E also proposed to the Commission on that day a change pursuant to Article 19(2)(4) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 in their member and alternate Member in the EACCC in line with Article 19(2)(i) of that Regulation.

- (12) Regarding the representation of airport operators in the NMB, the Airport Council International Europe (ACI Europe) proposed to the Commission on 1 July 2026 pursuant to Article 18(7) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 a change in their alternate member in the NMB in line with Article 18(3)(c) of that Regulation.

- (13) Regarding the representation of Eurocontrol in the EACCC, Eurocontrol proposed to the Commission on 16 June 2026 pursuant to Article 19(2)(2) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 a change in its representative in the EACCC in line with Article 19(2)(d) of that Regulation.

- (14) Regarding the representation of EASA in the EACCC, the Agency proposed to the Commission on 2 July 2026 pursuant to Article 19(2)(2) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/123 a change in its representative in the EACCC in line with Article 19(2)(c) of that Regulation.

- (15) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Single Sky Committee established by Article 48 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2803,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The persons listed in Annex I shall be appointed from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2028 as the members, alternates and observers to the Network Management Board, in the capacity referred to in that Annex.

#### Article 2

The persons listed in Annex II shall be appointed from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2028 as the members and alternates to the European Aviation Crisis Coordination Cell, in the capacity referred to in that Annex.

#### Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the 20th day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

It shall apply from 1 January 2027.

Done at Brussels, 29 October 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***
