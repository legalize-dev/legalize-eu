# Decision (EU) 2016/2164 of the European Central Bank of 30 November 2016 on the approval of the volume of coin issuance in 2017 (ECB/2016/43)

#### Article 2

##### Taking effect

This Decision shall take effect on the day of its notification to the addressees.

#### Article 3

##### Addressees

This Decision is addressed to the Member States whose currency is the euro.
