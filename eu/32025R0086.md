# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/86 of 20 January 2025 correcting Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2189 as regards the summary of the biocidal product characteristics

# COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2025/86 of 20 January 2025 correcting Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2189 as regards the summary of the biocidal product characteristics (Text with EEA relevance)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products (1), and in particular Article 44(5), first subparagraph, thereof,

Whereas:

(1) On 22 April 2024, the Standing Committee on Biocidal Products (the ‘Committee’) expressed a positive opinion on a draft act granting a Union authorisation for the single biocidal product ‘ClearKlens wipes based on IPA’ including the Annex thereto providing the summary of the biocidal product characteristics (the ‘SPC’).

(2) On 3 September 2024, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2189 (2) granting the authorisation was adopted and subsequently published, however without the SPC.

(3) Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2189 should therefore be corrected to include the SPC.

(4) In the interests of clarity and legal certainty, that correction should be made without delay and this Regulation should apply retroactively as the terms and conditions relating to the making available on the market and use of the single biocidal product ‘ClearKlens wipes based on IPA’ are defined in the SPC.

(5) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Biocidal Products delivered prior to the adoption of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2189,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

In Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2189, the text set out in the Annex to this Regulation is added as the Annex.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

It shall apply from 24 September 2024.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 20 January 2025.

**For the Commission
 The President
 Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
