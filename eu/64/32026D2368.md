# Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2368 of 26 October 2026 amending Decision (CFSP) 2022/2245 on an assistance measure under the European Peace Facility to support the Ukrainian Armed Forces trained by the European Union Military Assistance Mission in support of Ukraine with military equipment, and platforms, designed to deliver lethal force

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 28(1) and Article 41(2) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

- (1) On 14 November 2022, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2022/2245 [^ntr1-L_202602368EN.000101-E0001], which established an assistance measure to support the capacity building of the Armed Forces of Ukraine by the European Union Military Assistance Mission in support of Ukraine (EUMAM Ukraine) to allow the Armed Forces of Ukraine to defend the territorial integrity and sovereignty of Ukraine, as well as to protect the civilian population against the ongoing military aggression.

- (2) On 18 June 2026, the European Council reaffirmed its continued firm and unwavering support for Ukraine and reconfirmed the readiness of the Union and the Member States to contribute to robust and credible security guarantees for Ukraine, including through EUMAM Ukraine.

- (3) In this context, the assistance measure should continue supporting EUMAM Ukraine training efforts and, thus, the financial reference amount should be increased by an additional EUR 200 000 000 and the duration of the assistance measure extended to 80 months.

- (4) The financing of the support under this assistance measure will be reviewed by the Facility Committee established by Council Decision (CFSP) 2021/509 [^ntr2-L_202602368EN.000101-E0002] in due course, in line with Article 2(3) of that Decision, subject to a decision by the Council on a financial ceiling for the European Peace Facility regarding the next budgetary cycle in line with Article 73(11) of that Decision, without prejudice to the outcome of discussions thereunder,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

Decision (CFSP) 2022/2245 is amended as follows:

- (1) in Article 1, paragraphs 4 and 5 are replaced by the following:

> ‘4.   Upon full completion of the training or when EUMAM Ukraine is terminated, the custody of the ammunition, military equipment, and platforms, designed to deliver lethal force which were provided under the Assistance Measure shall be transferred to the beneficiary. Based on the needs of Ukraine, ammunition, military equipment, and platforms, designed to deliver lethal force, may be transferred to the beneficiary once they have been used in each training module.

> 5. The duration of the Assistance Measure shall be 80 months from the adoption of this Decision.’

;

- (2) in Article 2,

- (a) paragraph 1 is replaced by the following:

> ‘1.   The financial reference amount intended to cover the expenditure related to the Assistance Measure shall be EUR 400 000 000.’

;

- (b) paragraph 3 is replaced by the following:

> ‘3.   In accordance with Article 29(5) of Decision (CFSP) 2021/509, the administrator for operations may call for contributions following the adoption of this Decision, up to EUR 400 000 000. The funds called by the administrator for operations shall only be used to pay expenditure within the limits approved by the Committee established by Decision (CFSP) 2021/509 in the related budget corresponding to the Assistance Measure.’.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 26 October 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**M. HEYDON**
