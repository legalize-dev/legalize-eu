# Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2261 of 9 October 2026 amending Decision (CFSP) 2023/2135 concerning restrictive measures in view of activities undermining the stability and political transition of Sudan

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 29 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

- (1) On 9 October 2023, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2023/2135 [^ntr1-L_202602261EN.000101-E0001].

- (2) Decision (CFSP) 2023/2135 applies until 10 October 2026. On the basis of a review of that Decision, the restrictive measures set out therein should be extended until 10 October 2027.

- (3) The Council has concluded that the entries for three individuals listed in the Annex to Decision (CFSP) 2023/2135 should be updated.

- (4) Decision (CFSP) 2023/2135 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

In Article 9(1) of Decision (CFSP) 2023/2135, the date ‘10 October 2026’ is replaced by that of ‘10 October 2027’.

#### Article 2

The Annex to Decision (CFSP) 2023/2135 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Decision.

#### Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

Done at Luxembourg, 9 October 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**S. HARRIS**
