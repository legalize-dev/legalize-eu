# Commission Regulation (EC) No 2154/2005 of  23 December 2005  supplementing the Annex to Regulation (EC) No 2400/96 as regards the entry of a name in the  Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications ( Sidra de Asturias  or  Sidra d’Asturies )  [PDO]

# COMMISSION REGULATION (EC) No 2154/2005 of 23 December 2005 supplementing the Annex to Regulation (EC) No 2400/96 as regards the entry of a name in the ‘Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications’
 (‘Sidra de Asturias’ or ‘Sidra d’Asturies’) [PDO]

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EEC) No 2081/92 of 14 July 1992 on the protection of geographical indications and designations of origin for agricultural products and foodstuffs (1), and in particular Article 6(3) and (4) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) In accordance with Article 6(2) of Regulation (EEC) No 2081/92, the request from Spain to enter the name ‘Sidra de Asturias’ or ‘Sidra d’Asturies’ has been published in the Official Journal of the European Union (2).

(2) Since the Commission has received no statement of objection pursuant to Article 7 of Regulation (EEC) No 2081/92, this name must be entered in the ‘Register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications’,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name in the Annex to this Regulation is hereby added to the Annex to Commission Regulation (EC) No 2400/96 (3).

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 23 December 2005.

**For the Commission
 Mariann FISCHER BOEL
 Member of the Commission**
