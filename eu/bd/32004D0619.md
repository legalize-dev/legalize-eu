# 2004/619/ECCouncil Decision of 11 August 2004 modifying the Community import regime with respect to rice

#### Article 1

The tariff rate for husked rice (CN code 1006 20 ) shall be EUR 65/tonne.

The tariff rate for milled rice (CN code 1006 30 ) shall be EUR 175/tonne.

#### Article 2

To the extent necessary to permit the full application of this Decision by 1 September 2004, the Commission may derogate from Regulation (EC) No 1785/2003, in accordance with the procedure referred to in Article 3(2) of this Decision, until that Regulation is amended but at the latest until  30 June 2006.

#### Article 3

1. The Commission shall be assisted by the Management Committee for Cereals instituted by Article 25 of Council Regulation (EC) No 1784/2003 of 29 September 2003 on the common organisation of the market in cereals ([^E0003]).

2. Where reference is made to this paragraph, Articles 4 and 7 of Decision 1999/468/EC shall apply.

The period provided for in Article 4(3) of Decision 1999/468/EC shall be set at one month.

3. The Committee shall adopt its Rules of Procedure.
