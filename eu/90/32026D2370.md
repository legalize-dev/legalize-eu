# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2370 of 28 October 2026 on harmonised standards for household tumble dryers drafted in support of Regulation (EU) 2023/2533 and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2534

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr1-L_202602370EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 10(6) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) In accordance with Article 9(2) of Directive 2009/125/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr2-L_202602370EN.000101-E0002], a product which is in conformity with harmonised standards or parts thereof, the references of which have been published in the *Official Journal of the European Union*, is to be presumed to conform to the relevant requirements of the applicable implementing measure to which such standards relate.

- (2) By Implementing Decision of 1 December 2023 [^ntr3-L_202602370EN.000101-E0003] the Commission made a standardisation request to the European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardisation (Cenelec) for the revision of harmonised standards in support of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2534 [^ntr4-L_202602370EN.000101-E0004] and Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2533 [^ntr5-L_202602370EN.000101-E0005].

- (3) On the basis of the standardisation request Cenelec revised harmonised standard EN 61121:2013 and subsequently adopted amendment EN 61121:2013/A12:2025.

- (4) The Commission has assessed whether the standards EN 61121:2013 and EN 61121:2013/A12:2025 drafted by Cenelec comply with the standardisation request.

- (5) Standards EN 61121:2013 and EN 61121:2013/A12:2025 satisfy the requirements which they aim to cover and which are set out in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2534 and Regulation (EU) 2023/2533. It is therefore appropriate to publish the references of those standards in the *Official Journal of the European Union.*

- (6) In the interest of clarity and rationality, a complete list of references of harmonised standards drafted in support of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2534 and Regulation (EU) 2023/2533 and satisfying the requirements they aim to cover should be published in a single act.

- (7) Standard EN 61121:2013 includes references to harmonised standard EN 60704-2-6:2012 regarding the determination of airborne acoustical noise from household tumble dryers. EN 60704-2-6:2012 was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* by Commission Communication 2014/C 149/01 [^ntr6-L_202602370EN.000101-E0006]. Normative references from a standard to another standard can be made to either harmonised standards or to non-harmonised standards, thus making unnecessary the publication of EN 60704-2-6:2012 as reference of a harmonised standard. In the interest of clarity and rationality, the reference of harmonised standard EN 60704-2-6:2012 should be withdrawn from the *Official Journal of the European Union* and Communication 2014/C 149/01 should be repealed.

- (8) Compliance with a harmonised standard confers a presumption of conformity with the corresponding relevant requirements set out in Union harmonisation legislation from the date of publication of the reference of such standard in the *Official Journal of the European Union*. This Decision should therefore enter into force on the day of its publication,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The references of harmonised standards for household tumble dryers and drafted in support of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2534 and Regulation (EU) 2023/2533, listed in the Annex to this Decision, are hereby published in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

#### Article 2

The reference of harmonised standard EN 60704-2-6:2012 is hereby withdrawn from the *Official Journal of the European Union* and Communication 2014/C 149/01 is repealed.

#### Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union.*

Done at Brussels, 28 October 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
