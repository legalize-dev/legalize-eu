# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2400 of 20 July 2026 amending Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council by deleting the measures for the Northern Midsea Bank

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 on the conservation of fisheries resources and the protection of marine ecosystems through technical measures, amending Council Regulations (EC) No 1967/2006, (EC) No 1224/2009 and Regulations (EU) No 1380/2013, (EU) 2016/1139, (EU) 2018/973, (EU) 2019/472 and (EU) 2019/1022 of the European Parliament and of the Council, and repealing Council Regulations (EC) No 894/97, (EC) No 850/98, (EC) No 2549/2000, (EC) No 254/2002, (EC) No 812/2004 and (EC) No 2187/2005 [^ntr1-L_202602400EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 2(2) and Article 15(2) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Annex XIII to Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 lays down rules at regional level regarding mitigation measures to reduce incidental catches of sensitive species including cetaceans, by detailing restricted areas, periods and gear limitations.

- (2) Point 3.1 of Part A of Annex XIII to that Regulation provides for measures, introduced by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/303 [^ntr2-L_202602400EN.000101-E0002], to reduce incidental catches of the Baltic Proper harbour porpoise (*Phocoena phocoena*) in the Baltic Sea. Those measures included, inter alia, the closure of the marine protected area Northern Midsea Bank (Norra Midsjöbanken) to all fisheries except for pots, fish traps and longlines.

- (3) Article 15(3) of Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 allows Member States with a direct management interest to submit joint recommendations for the purpose of adopting delegated acts concerning regional technical measures set out in the Annexes to that Regulation.

- (4) On 20 November 2025, Sweden, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland and Finland submitted a joint recommendation requesting to repeal the measures that apply to the Northern Midsea Bank, since more stringent fisheries conservation measures were proposed by those Member States in a joint recommendation submitted for that same area in February 2025, under Article 11 of Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr3-L_202602400EN.000101-E0003].

- (5) The joint recommendation of February 2025 proposed to prohibit all fisheries in that area. The proposed measures were assessed positively by the Scientific, Technical and Economic Committee for Fisheries in the Plenary meeting of 7 to 11 July 2025 [^ntr4-L_202602400EN.000101-E0004].

- (6) Given that those proposed fisheries conservation measures would overlap and contradict the measures currently under point 3.1 of Part A of Annex XIII to Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 applicable to the same area, the latter measures should therefore be repealed once the conservation measures in the marine protected area of Norra Midsjöbanken, to be adopted under Article 11 of Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013, start applying.

- (7) Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Point 3.1 of Part A of Annex XIII to Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 is deleted.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

It shall apply from 19 November 2026.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 20 July 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
