THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 883/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004 on the coordination of social security systems [^ntr1-C_202606026EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 75 thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) By Decision of 10 October 2025 [^ntr2-C_202606026EN.000101-E0002], the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee for the Coordination of Social Security Systems for the period from 20 October 2025 to 19 October 2030.

- (2) A seat as alternate member in the government representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Agnes SÖRENSEN.

- (3) Sweden has nominated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

Ms Martina AXMIN is appointed alternate member of the Advisory Committee for the Coordination of Social Security Systems in place of Ms Agnes SÖRENSEN for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 19 October 2030.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 10 November 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**S. HARRIS**
