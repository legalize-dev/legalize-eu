# Commission Regulation (EU) 2022/943 of 17 June 2022 correcting certain language versions of Annex I to Regulation (EC) No 2195/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the Common Procurement Vocabulary (CPV) (Text with EEA relevance)

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20.6.2022 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 164/6 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 2195/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 November 2002 on the Common Procurement Vocabulary (CPV) [^ntr1-L_2022164EN.01000601-E0001], and in particular Article 2 thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) The Croatian and Estonian language versions of Regulation (EC) No 2195/2002 contain an error in Annex I, in the table ‘Main Vocabulary’, second column as regards the Common Procurement Vocabulary (CPV) code 79530000-8.

- (2) The Croatian and Estonian language versions of Annex I to Regulation (EC) No 2195/2002 should therefore be corrected accordingly. The other language versions are not affected.

- (3) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Advisory Committee for Public Contracts,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

*(does not concern the English language)*

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 17 June 2022.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
