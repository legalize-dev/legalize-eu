THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 28(1) and Article 41(2) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

- (1) On 5 November 2024, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2024/2840 on an assistance measure under the European Peace Facility (EPF) to strengthen the capacities of the Navy of the Republic of the Congo in the framework of the Yaoundé Architecture [^ntr1-L_202602507EN.000101-E0001].

- (2) On 28 May 2026, the Political and Security Committee supported the intended reallocation of available funds in the EPF assistance measures pillar in 2026, including the increase of EUR 5 000 000 in the financial reference amount to be paid to the Navy of the Republic of the Congo.

- (3) The delivery of part of the additional support to the Navy of the Republic of the Congo will take place after the end date of the implementation period of Decision (CFSP) 2024/2840, requiring an extension of the implementation period of the relevant assistance measure by 12 months, from 5 November 2027 to 4 November 2028.

- (4) Decision (CFSP) 2024/2840 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

Decision (CFSP) 2024/2840 is amended as follows:

- (1) in Article 1, paragraph 4 is replaced by the following:

> ‘4.   The duration of the assistance measure shall be 48 months from the date of adoption of this Decision.’

;

- (2) in Article 2, paragraph 1 is replaced by the following:

> ‘1.   The financial reference amount intended to cover the expenditure related to the assistance measure shall be EUR 10 000 000.’.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 9 November 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**S. HARRIS**
