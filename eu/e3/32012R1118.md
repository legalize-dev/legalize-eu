# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1118/2012 of 28 November 2012 entering a name in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications (东山白芦笋 (Dongshan Bai Lu Sun) (PGI))

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30.11.2012 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 330/12 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 of 20 March 2006 on the protection of geographical indications and designations of origin for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_2012330EN.01001201-E0001], and in particular the first subparagraph of Article 7(4) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 6(2) of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006, China’s application to register the name ‘

[Image omitted; see official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32012R1118)

(Dongshan Bai Lu Sun)’ was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr2-L_2012330EN.01001201-E0002].

- (2) As no statement of objection under Article 7 of Regulation (EC) No 510/2006 has been received by the Commission, that name should therefore be entered in the register,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name contained in the Annex to this Regulation is hereby entered in the register.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 28 November 2012.

***For the Commission, On behalf of the President*,**

**Dacian CIOLOȘ**

***Member of the Commission***
