# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/269 of 4 December 2020 amending Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/427 as regards the date of application of the amendments to certain detailed production rules for organic products in Annex II to Regulation (EU) 2018/848 of the European Parliament and of the Council (Text with EEA relevance)

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22.2.2021 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 60/24 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2018/848 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 30 May 2018 on organic production and labelling of organic products and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 834/2007 [^ntr1-L_2021060EN.01002401-E0001], and in particular Articles 12(2)(e), 14(2)(c) and 15(2)(a) and (d) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Due to the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic and the related public health crisis, Regulation (EU) 2020/1693 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr2-L_2021060EN.01002401-E0002] postponed the date of application of Regulation (EU) 2018/848 by one year, together with other related dates referred to in Regulation (EU) 2018/848.

- (2) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/427 [^ntr3-L_2021060EN.01002401-E0003] amended Regulation (EU) 2018/848 as regards certain detailed production rules for organic products. In the interest of legal certainty, those production rules should apply from the date of application of Regulation (EU) 2018/848.

- (3) Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/427 should therefore be amended accordingly.

- (4) As this Regulation needs to apply as a matter of urgency, it should enter into force on the day of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union* and apply from 1 January 2021,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

In Article 2 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/427, the second paragraph is replaced by the following:

> ‘It shall apply from 1 January 2022.’

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

It shall apply from 1 January 2021.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 4 December 2020.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
