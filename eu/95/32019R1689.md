# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1689 of 29 May 2019 correcting the Romanian language version of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/1229 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 909/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to regulatory technical standards on settlement discipline (Text with EEA relevance)

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10.10.2019 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 259/1 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 909/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on improving securities settlement in the European Union and on central securities depositories and amending Directives 98/26/EC and 2014/65/EU and Regulation (EU) No 236/2012 [^ntr1-L_2019259EN.01000101-E0001] and in particular Article 6(5) and Article 7(15) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) The Romanian language version of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/1229 [^ntr2-L_2019259EN.01000101-E0002] contains an error in Article 42 as regards the entry into force of the act.

- (2) Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/1229 should therefore be corrected accordingly. The other language versions are not affected,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

*(does not concern the English language)*

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 29 May 2019.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Jean-Claude JUNCKER**
