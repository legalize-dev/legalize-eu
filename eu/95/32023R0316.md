# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/316 of 6 February 2023 entering a name in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications (‘Goranski medun’ (PDO))

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13.2.2023 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 43/7 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 November 2012 on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_2023043EN.01000701-E0001], and in particular Article 52(2) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Pursuant to Article 50(2)(a) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012, Croatia’s application to register the name ‘Goranski medun’ was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr2-L_2023043EN.01000701-E0002].

- (2) As no statement of opposition under Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 has been received by the Commission, the name ‘Goranski medun’ should therefore be entered in the register,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name ‘Goranski medun’ (PDO) is hereby entered in the register.

The name specified in the first paragraph denotes a product in Class 1.4. Other products of animal origin (eggs, honey, various dairy products except butter, etc.), as listed in Annex XI to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 668/2014 [^ntr3-L_2023043EN.01000701-E0003].

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 6 February 2023.

***For the Commission,***

***On behalf of the President,***

**Janusz WOJCIECHOWSKI**

***Member of the Commission***
