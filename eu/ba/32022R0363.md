# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/363 of 24 January 2022 amending and correcting Annex IX to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/405 as regards the lists of third countries or regions thereof authorised for the entry into the Union of certain fishery products (Text with EEA relevance)

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2022/363

of 2 March 2022

amending and correcting Annex IX to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/405 as regards the lists of third countries or regions thereof authorised for the entry into the Union of certain fishery products

(Text with EEA relevance)

#### Article 1

Annex IX to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/405 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

## ANNEX

Annex IX to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/405 is amended as follows:

- a) the entry for United Arab Emirates is replaced by the following:
   ‘AE
    United Arab Emirates
    Aquaculture: finfish and caviar (product of finfish)
    Only raw material either from Member States or from other third countries approved for imports of such raw material into the Union’

- b) the entry for Belarus is replaced by the following:
   ‘BY
    Belarus
    Aquaculture: finfish and caviar (product of finfish)’

- c) the entry for Canada is replaced by the following:
   ‘CA
    Canada [^E0001]
    (<sup>*1</sup>)
    These third countries or regions thereof can export all fishery products (finfish, caviar (product of finfish) and crustaceans).’

- d) the entry for Switzerland is replaced by the following:
   ‘CH
    Switzerland [^E0002]
    Aquaculture: finfish and caviar (product of finfish)
    (<sup>*1</sup>)
    In accordance with the Agreement of 21 June 1999 between the European Community and the Swiss Confederation on trade in agricultural products (OJ L 114, 30.4.2002, p. 132).’

- e) the entry for China is replaced by the following:
   ‘CN
    China [^E0003]
    (<sup>*1</sup>)
    These third countries or regions thereof can export all fishery products (finfish, caviar (product of finfish) and crustaceans).’

- f) the entry for Israel is replaced by the following:
   ‘IL
    Israel [^E0004]
    Aquaculture: finfish and caviar (product of finfish)
    (<sup>*1</sup>)
    Hereinafter understood as the State of Israel, excluding the territories under Israeli administration since June 1967, namely the Golan Heights, the Gaza Strip, East Jerusalem and the rest of the West Bank.’

- g) the entry for Moldova is replaced by the following:
   ‘MD
    Moldova
    Aquaculture: finfish and caviar (product of finfish)’

- h) the following entry is inserted between the entries for New Zealand and Panama:
   ‘OM
    Oman
    Only wild catch’

- i) the entry for Turkey is replaced by the following:
   ‘TR
    Turkey
    Aquaculture: finfish and caviar (product of finfish)’

- j) the entry for the United States is replaced by the following:
   ‘US
    United States [^E0005]
    (<sup>*1</sup>)
    These third countries or regions thereof can export all fishery products (finfish, caviar (product of finfish) and crustaceans).’

- k) the entry for Uruguay is replaced by the following:
   ‘UY
    Uruguay
    Aquaculture: finfish and caviar (product of finfish)’
