# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2306 of 16 October 2026 amending Implementing Decision (EU, Euratom) 2018/194 as regards the models for statements of accounts for entitlements to traditional own resources and the form for reports on irrecoverable amounts

|  |  |  |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 609/2014 of 26 May 2014 on the methods and procedure for making available the traditional, VAT and GNI-based own resources and on the measures to meet cash requirements [^ntr1-L_202602306EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 6(4), fourth subparagraph, and Article 13(3), third subparagraph, thereof,

After consulting the Advisory Committee on Own Resources,

Whereas:

- (1) All amounts qualified as traditional own resources within the meaning of Article 2(1), point (a), of Council Decision (EU, Euratom) 2020/2053 [^ntr2-L_202602306EN.000101-E0002] are to be credited to Commission’s accounts in accordance with Article 9(1) of Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 609/2014. In accordance with Article 6(4), first subparagraph, and Article 13(3) of that Regulation, Member States are to send to the Commission statements of those accounts for the entitlements to own resources and to provide it with a report on irrecoverable amounts. For the sake of comprehensive accounting and transparency, Commission Implementing Decision (EU, Euratom) 2018/194 [^ntr3-L_202602306EN.000101-E0003] lays down in Annexes I and III thereto, respectively, models for the statement of established entitlements to the European Union’s own resources, for the statement of such entitlements that are yet to be recovered, and in Annex V thereto the form for the report on irrecoverable amounts.

- (2) Regulation (EU) 2026/2108 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr4-L_202602306EN.000101-E0004] establishes a Union handling fee to apply in respect of trade with third countries. This fee is to consist of a fixed amount per item for the services to be rendered for handling a request for placing goods under the release for free circulation procedure, where those goods are sold in distance sales.

- (3) That Union handling fee is to be qualified as a traditional own resource within the meaning of Article 2(1), point (a), of Council Decision (EU, Euratom) 2020/2053 [^ntr5-L_202602306EN.000101-E0005]. The Union handling fee is not currently included in the categories of customs duties and other duties in the models for statements of accounts and in the form for the report on irrecoverable amounts as set out in Implementing Decision (EU, Euratom) 2018/194. Therefore, a dedicated entry line in those models would help ensure that the Union handling fee is recorded transparently and accurately.

- (4) Annexes I, III and V to Implementing Decision (EU, Euratom) 2018/194 should therefore be amended accordingly, to incorporate a separate reporting line for the Union handling fee in the models for statements of accounts and the form for the report of irrecoverable amounts.

- (5) In the interest of legal certainty, the models for statements of accounts and the form for the report on irrecoverable amounts as amended by this Decision should apply from the date from which the Union handling fee provided for in Regulation (EU) 2026/2108 applies. Pursuant to Article 287(4) of Regulation (EU) 2026/2108, the Union handling fee applies from the tenth day after the entry into force of the delegated act adopted pursuant to Article 20(10) of that Regulation. This Decision should therefore apply from the same date,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

1. Annexes I and III to Implementing Decision (EU, Euratom) 2018/194 are replaced by the texts in the Annex I to this Decision.

2. Annex V to Implementing Decision (EU, Euratom) 2018/194 is amended in accordance with Annex II to this Decision.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union.*

This Decision shall apply from the date of application of the Union handling fee as referred to in Article 287(4) of Regulation (EU) 2026/2108.

Done at Brussels, 16 October 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***
