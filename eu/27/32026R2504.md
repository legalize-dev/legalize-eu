# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2504 of 12 November 2026 cancelling the Union authorisation for the single biocidal product Sterillium liquid in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1867

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products [^ntr1-L_202602504EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 49 thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) On 10 September 2025, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1867 [^ntr2-L_202602504EN.000101-E0002] granted a Union authorisation with the authorisation number EU-0033788-0000 to the authorisation holder BODE Chemie GmbH for the making available on the market and use of the same single biocidal product containing Propan-1-ol and Propan-2-ol ‘Sterillium liquid’ in accordance with Article 44(5), first subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012.

- (2) On 19 June 2026, BODE Chemie GmbH submitted to the European Chemicals Agency (‘the Agency’) a request to cancel that Union authorisation in accordance with Article 49 of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012. The request was recorded under case number BC-QX119221-18 in the Register for Biocidal Products.

- (3) On 23 June 2026, BODE Chemie GmbH had submitted a reasoning for the request for cancellation, providing that it had decided to discontinue the production of ‘Sterillium liquid’ based on commercial considerations.

- (4) On 24 June 2026, the Agency forwarded the reasoned request to the Commission.

- (5) It is therefore appropriate to cancel the Union authorisation for the single biocidal product ‘Sterillium liquid’ at the reasoned request of the authorisation holder and consequently to repeal Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1867.

- (6) It is also appropriate to grant a period of grace for the making available on the market and the use of the existing stocks of ‘Sterillium liquid’, in accordance with Article 52 of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The Union authorisation for the single biocidal product ‘Sterillium liquid’ granted by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1867 to BODE Chemie GmbH with the authorisation number EU-0033788-0000 is cancelled.

#### Article 2

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1867 is repealed.

#### Article 3

The biocidal product ‘Sterillium liquid’ shall no longer be made available on the market from 3 June 2027 and existing stocks of such biocidal product shall no longer be used from 3 December 2027.

#### Article 4

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 12 November 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
