# Council Decision of 14 July 2023 amending Decision 98/481/EC approving the external auditors of the European Central Bank 2023/C 275/08

#### Article 1

Article 1 of Decision 98/481/EC is replaced by the following:

> ‘Article 1

> Baker Tilly GmbH & Co. KG Wirtschaftsprüfungsgesellschaft is hereby approved as the external auditors of the ECB for the financial years 2023 and 2024.’.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall take effect on the day of its notification.

#### Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the ECB.
