# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2584 of 17 November 2026 excluding from European Union financing certain expenditure incurred by certain Member States under the European Agricultural Guarantee Fund (EAGF) and under the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD) (notified under document C(2026) 8075)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2021/2116 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2 December 2021 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 [^ntr1-L_202602584EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Articles 55 and 104 thereof,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 352/78, (EC) No 165/94, (EC) No 2799/98, (EC) No 814/2000, (EC) No 1290/2005 and (EC) No 485/2008 [^ntr2-L_202602584EN.000101-E0002], and in particular Article 52 thereof,

After consulting the Committee on the Agricultural Funds,

Whereas:

- (1) Article 4(1), point (b), Article 5, Article 7(3), Articles 9, 17, 21 and 34, Article 35(4), Articles 36, 37, 38, 40 to 43, 51, 52, 54, 56, 59, 63, 64, 67, 68, 70 to 75, 77, 91 to 97, 99 and 100, Article 102(2) and Articles 110 and 111 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 continue to apply, as regards the European Agricultural Guarantee Fund (EAGF) and the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD), in relation to expenditure as referred to in Article 104(1), second subparagraph, point (a), of Regulation (EU) 2021/2116.

- (2) In accordance with Article 52 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 and Article 55 of Regulation (EU) 2021/2116, the Commission is to carry out the necessary verifications, communicate to the Member States the results of those verifications, take note of the comments of the Member States, initiate a bilateral discussion so that an agreement may be reached with the Member States in question and formally communicate its conclusions to them.

- (3) In accordance with Article 52(3), second subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 and Article 34(3), third subparagraph, and Article 40 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 908/2014 [^ntr3-L_202602584EN.000101-E0003], and Article 55(3), second subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) 2021/2116 and Article 37(3), third subparagraph, and Article 43 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/128 [^ntr4-L_202602584EN.000101-E0004], some Member States made use of the conciliation procedure. The Commission has examined the reports issued by the Conciliation Body on the outcome of these conciliation procedures.

- (4) In accordance with Regulations (EU) No 1306/2013 and (EU) 2021/2116, only agricultural expenditure which has been incurred in a way that has not infringed Union law may be financed, subject to, as regards expenditure incurred under Regulation (EU) 2021/2116, certain limitations in Article 55(1), second and third subparagraphs of that Regulation.

- (5) In light of the verifications carried out, the outcome of the bilateral discussions and the conciliation procedures, part of the expenditure declared by certain Member States does not fulfil this requirement and therefore cannot be financed under the EAGF and the EAFRD.

- (6) The Commission should indicate the amounts that are not recognised as being chargeable to the EAGF and the EAFRD. Those amounts do not relate to expenditure incurred more than 24 months before the Commission's written notification of the results of the verifications to those Member States, except where otherwise provided for in Article 52(4), points (b) and (c), and Article 52(5) of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 and Article 55(4), points (b) and (c), and Article 55(5) of Regulation (EU) 2021/2116.

- (7) The amounts excluded from Union financing by the present Decision should also take into account any reductions or suspensions in accordance with Article 41 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 due to the fact that such reductions or suspensions are of a provisional nature and without prejudice to decisions taken pursuant to Articles 51 or 52 of that Regulation.

- (8) As regards the cases covered by this decision, the Commission notified the assessment of the amounts to be excluded on grounds of non-compliance with Union law to the Member States concerned in the summary report on the subject [^ntr5-L_202602584EN.000101-E0005].

- (9) This Decision is without prejudice to any financial conclusions that the Commission may draw from the judgments of the Court of Justice of the European Union in cases pending on 31 May 2026,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The amounts set out in the Annex and related to expenditure incurred by the Member States’ accredited paying agencies and declared under the EAGF or the EAFRD shall be excluded from Union financing.

#### Article 2

This Decision is addressed to the Kingdom of Belgium, the Republic of Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, Ireland, the Hellenic Republic, the Kingdom of Spain, the French Republic, the Republic of Croatia, the Italian Republic, Hungary, the Kingdom of the Netherlands, the Portuguese Republic, Romania and the Slovak Republic.

Done at Brussels, 17 November 2026.

***For the Commission***

**Christophe HANSEN**

***Member of the Commission***
