THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market and repealing Council Directives 79/117/EEC and 91/414/EEC [^ntr1-L_202501489EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 17, first paragraph, thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 200/2013 [^ntr2-L_202501489EN.000101-E0002] approved the active substance ametoctradin until 31 July 2023.

- (2) Commission Directive 2008/113/EC [^ntr3-L_202501489EN.000101-E0003] included *Beauveria bassiana* strains ATCC 74040 and GHA as an active substance in Annex I to Council Directive 91/414/EEC [^ntr4-L_202501489EN.000101-E0004] until 30 April 2019.

- (3) Commission Directive 2011/6/EU [^ntr5-L_202501489EN.000101-E0005] included buprofezin as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 January 2021.

- (4) Commission Directive 2006/39/EC [^ntr6-L_202501489EN.000101-E0006] included clodinafop as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 January 2017.

- (5) Commission Directive 2009/37/EC [^ntr7-L_202501489EN.000101-E0007] included copper compounds as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 30 November 2016 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1981 [^ntr8-L_202501489EN.000101-E0008] renewed the approval of the active substance as candidate for substitution until 31 December 2025.

- (6) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 22/2013 [^ntr9-L_202501489EN.000101-E0009] approved the active substance cyflumetofen until 31 May 2023.

- (7) Commission Directive 2005/53/EC [^ntr10-L_202501489EN.000101-E0010] included daminozide as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 28 February 2016.

- (8) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2084 [^ntr11-L_202501489EN.000101-E0011] approved the active substance flupyradifurone until 9 December 2025.

- (9) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 368/2013 [^ntr12-L_202501489EN.000101-E0012] approved the active substance *Helicoverpa armigera nucleopolyhedrovirus* until 31 May 2023.

- (10) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2085 [^ntr13-L_202501489EN.000101-E0013] approved the active substance mandestrobin until 9 December 2025.

- (11) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 188/2013 [^ntr14-L_202501489EN.000101-E0014] approved the active substance mandipropamid until 31 July 2023.

- (12) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 359/2012 [^ntr15-L_202501489EN.000101-E0015] approved the active substance metam until 30 June 2022.

- (13) Commission Directive 2004/30/EC [^ntr16-L_202501489EN.000101-E0016] included pyraclostrobin as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 May 2014.

- (14) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/2198 [^ntr17-L_202501489EN.000101-E0017] approved the active substance rescalure until 18 December 2025.

- (15) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 367/2013 [^ntr18-L_202501489EN.000101-E0018] approved the active substance *Spodoptera littoralis nucleopolyhedrovirus* until 31 May 2023.

- (16) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 917/2014 [^ntr19-L_202501489EN.000101-E0019] approved the active substance *Streptomyces lydicus* strain WYEC 108 until 31 December 2024.

- (17) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1238/2012 [^ntr20-L_202501489EN.000101-E0020] approved the active substance *Trichoderma asperellum* strain T34 until 31 May 2023.

- (18) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 17/2013 [^ntr21-L_202501489EN.000101-E0021] approved the active substance *Trichoderma atroviride* strain I-1237 until 31 May 2023.

- (19) The active substances *Beauveria bassiana* strains ATCC 74040 and GHA, buprofezin, clodinafop, daminozide and pyraclostrobin were included in Part A of the Annex to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 540/2011 [^ntr22-L_202501489EN.000101-E0022]. The active substances ametoctradin, cyflumetofen, flupyradifurone, *Helicoverpa armigera nucleopolyhedrovirus*, mandestrobin, mandipropamid, metam, rescalure, *Spodoptera littoralis nucleopolyhedrovirus*, *Streptomyces lydicus* strain WYEC 108, *Trichoderma asperellum* strain T34 and *Trichoderma atroviride* strain I-1237 were included in Part B, and the active substance copper compounds was included in Part E of the Annex to that Regulation.

- (20) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/2007 [^ntr23-L_202501489EN.000101-E0023] extended the approval period of the active substance *Streptomyces lydicus* strain WYEC 108 until 31 December 2025.

- (21) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/689 [^ntr24-L_202501489EN.000101-E0024] extended the approval period of the active substance *Beauveria bassiana* strains ATCC 74040 and GHA until 30 September 2025, and the approval period of the active substance clodinafop until 15 December 2025.

- (22) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/918 [^ntr25-L_202501489EN.000101-E0025] extended the approval periods of the active substances cyflumetofen, *Helicoverpa armigera nucleopolyhedrovirus*, *Spodoptera littoralis nucleopolyhedrovirus*, *Trichoderma asperellum* strain T34 and *Trichoderma atroviride* strain I-1237 until 31 October 2025, the approval period of the active substance metam until 30 November 2025, and the approval periods of the active substances ametoctradin and mandipropamid until 31 December 2025.

- (23) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1757 [^ntr26-L_202501489EN.000101-E0026] extended the approval period of the active substance daminozide until 15 September 2025.

- (24) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/324 [^ntr27-L_202501489EN.000101-E0027] extended the approval period of the active substance pyraclostrobin until 15 September 2025, and the approval period of the active substance buprofezin until 15 December 2025.

- (25) Applications for the respective renewals of the approval of the active substances ametoctradin, buprofezin, cyflumetofen, daminozide, *Helicoverpa armigera nucleopolyhedrovirus*, mandipropamid, metam, pyraclostrobin, *Spodoptera littoralis nucleopolyhedrovirus*, *Trichoderma asperellum* strain T34 and *Trichoderma atroviride* strain I-1237 were submitted in accordance with Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 844/2012 [^ntr28-L_202501489EN.000101-E0028].
