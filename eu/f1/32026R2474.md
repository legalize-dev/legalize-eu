# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2474 of 6 November 2026 granting a Union authorisation for the biocidal product Product 3 in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products [^ntr1-L_202602474EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 44(5), first subparagraph, thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) On 30 June 2022, Zimmer Reinigungstechnik GmbH submitted an application to the European Chemicals Agency (‘the Agency’) in accordance with Article 43(1) of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 and Article 4 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 414/2013 [^ntr2-L_202602474EN.000101-E0002] for Union authorisation of the same product, as referred to in Article 1 of Implementing Regulation (EU) No 414/2013, named ‘Product 3’, of product-type 1, as described in Annex V to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012. The application was recorded under case number BC-UG078203-36 in the Register for Biocidal Products. The application referred to the biocidal product family ‘ECA Disinfect skin Product family 7’ authorised by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/750 [^ntr3-L_202602474EN.000101-E0003], with authorisation number EU-0035043-0000.

- (2) The same product ‘Product 3’ contains active chlorine released from hypochlorous acid as the active substance, included in the Union list of approved active substances referred to in Article 9(2) of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 for product-type 1.

- (3) On 2 July 2025, the Agency submitted to the Commission its opinion [^ntr4-L_202602474EN.000101-E0004] in accordance with Article 6 of Implementing Regulation (EU) No 414/2013, and on 14 July 2025, the draft summary of product characteristics (‘SPC’) of ‘Product 3’, in accordance with Article 44(4) of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012.

- (4) In the opinion, the Agency concludes that the proposed differences between the same product ‘Product 3’ and the related reference biocidal product ‘ECA Disinfect skin 1000’, which is a family member of ‘ECA Disinfect skin Product family 7,’ are limited to information which can be the subject of an administrative change in accordance with Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 354/2013 [^ntr5-L_202602474EN.000101-E0005], and that based on the assessment of the related reference biocidal product ‘ECA Disinfect skin 1000’ and subject to compliance with the draft SPC, the same product ‘Product 3’ meets the conditions laid down in Article 19(1) of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012.

- (5) The Commission concurs with the opinion of the Agency and considers it therefore appropriate to grant a Union authorisation for the same product ‘Product 3’.

- (6) The expiry date of the authorisation should be aligned with the expiry date of the authorisation of the related reference biocidal product ‘ECA Disinfect skin 1000’.

- (7) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Biocidal Products,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

A Union authorisation with authorisation number EU-0035170-0000 is hereby granted to Zimmer Reinigungstechnik GmbH for the making available on the market and use of the same product ‘Product 3’ in accordance with the summary of the product characteristics set out in the Annex.

The Union authorisation is valid from 29 November 2026 until 31 March 2036.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 6 November 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**

### 5. GENERAL DIRECTIONS FOR USE

#### 5.1.   **Instructions for use**

Comply with instructions for use.

#### 5.2.   **Risk mitigation measures**

No risk mitigation measures needed.

#### 5.3.   **Particulars of likely direct or indirect effects, first aid instructions and emergency measures to protect the environment**

IF INHALED: If symptoms occur call a POISON CENTRE or a doctor.

IF SWALLOWED: If symptoms occur call a POISON CENTRE or a doctor.

IF ON SKIN: If irritation occurs wash with water and seek medical advice. In cases of unintentional skin exposure: wash with water.

IF IN EYES: If symptoms occur rinse with water. Remove contact lenses, if present and easy to do. Call a POISON CENTRE or a doctor.

If medical advice is needed, have product container or label at hand.

#### 5.4.   **Instructions for safe disposal of the product and its packaging**

Dispose of content/container in accordance with local/national/international regulations.

#### 5.5.   **Conditions of storage and shelf-life of the product under normal conditions of storage**

Shelf life: 18 months.

Must be stored at a temperature of maximum 20 °C.

Protect from frost.

Protect from light.

Keep out of reach of children and non-target animals/pets

### 6. OTHER INFORMATION
