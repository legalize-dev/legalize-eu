# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2579 of 20 November 2026 terminating the anti-dumping investigation concerning imports of urea originating in Russia

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2016/1036 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2016 on protection against dumped imports from countries not members of the European Union (‘the basic Regulation’) [^ntr1-L_202602579EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 9(1) thereof,

Whereas:

### 1. **PROCEDURE**

#### 1.1.   **Initiation**

- (1) On 11 August 2025, the European Commission (‘the Commission’) received a complaint pursuant to Article 5 of the basic Regulation, alleging that imports of urea originating in Russia (‘the country concerned’) are being dumped and are thereby causing injury to the Union industry.

- (2) The complaint was lodged by Fertilizers Europe (‘the complainant’), an association representing European manufacturers of urea. The complaint was made on behalf of the Union industry of urea in the sense of Article 5(4) of the basic Regulation.

- (3) On 25 September 2025, the Commission initiated an anti-dumping investigation on the basis of Article 5 of the basic Regulation. It published a Notice of Initiation in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr2-L_202602579EN.000101-E0002] (‘the Notice of Initiation’).

#### 1.2.   **Registration**

- (4) The Commission made imports of the product concerned subject to registration by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2490 [^ntr3-L_202602579EN.000101-E0003] (‘the registration Regulation’).

### 2. **WITHDRAWAL OF THE COMPLAINT**

- (5) In a letter dated 9 October 2026, the complainant withdrew its complaint.

- (6) In accordance with Article 9(1) of the basic Regulation, the proceeding may be terminated, unless such termination would not be in the Union interest. The investigation had not brought to light any considerations demonstrating that such termination would not be in the Union interest.

### 3. **CONCLUSION AND DISCLOSURE**

- (7) The Commission, therefore, concluded that the proceeding should be terminated.

- (8) Interested parties were informed accordingly and were granted an opportunity to submit comments.

- (9) The Commission received no comments which would lead to the conclusion that such termination would not be in the Union interest.

- (10) This Decision is in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 15(1) of the basic anti-dumping Regulation,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The anti-dumping investigation concerning imports of urea, whether or not in an aqueous solution, whether or not with additives and with varying concentrations of nitrogen depending on the dilution of the aqueous solution, originating in Russia currently falling under CN codes classified under CN codes 3102 10 12 , 3102 10 15 , 3102 10 19 and 3102 10 90 is hereby terminated.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

Done at Brussels, 20 November 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***
