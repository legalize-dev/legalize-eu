# Council Decision (EU) 2026/2555 of 9 November 2026 on the position to be taken on behalf of the European Union within the EU-Central America Association Council concerning an amendment to Decision No 2/2020 of the EU-Central America Association Council to replace the Explanatory Note to Article 19 of Annex II to the Agreement establishing an Association between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and Central America on the other and to insert Explanatory Notes to Articles 22 and 27 of Annex II to that Agreement

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 207(4), first subparagraph, in conjunction with Article 218(9) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the European Commission,

Whereas:

- (1) The Agreement establishing an Association between the European Union and its Member States, on the one hand, and Central America on the other (the ‘Agreement’) was concluded by the Union by means of Council Decision (EU) 2024/1156 [^ntr1-L_202602555EN.000101-E0001] and has been applied provisionally since 1 August 2013 between the Union, Nicaragua, Honduras and Panama, since 1 October 2013 between those parties and El Salvador and Costa Rica, and since 1 December 2013 between the Union, Nicaragua, Honduras, Panama, El Salvador and Costa Rica, of the one part, and Guatemala, of the other part.

- (2) Pursuant to Article 37 of Annex II to the Agreement, the Association Council (the ‘EU-Central America Association Council’) is able to approve Explanatory Notes regarding the interpretation, application and administration of that Annex.

- (3) The EU-Central America Association Council is scheduled to adopt a Decision amending Decision No 2/2020 of the EU-Central America Association Council [^ntr2-L_202602555EN.000101-E0002] to replace the Explanatory Note to Article 19 of Annex II to the Agreement and to insert Explanatory Notes to Articles 22 and 27 of that Annex.

- (4) The Decision of the EU-Central America Association Council will, if adopted, produce legal effects for the Union.

- (5) It is therefore necessary to establish the position to be taken within the EU-Central America Association Council concerning the amendment of Decision No 2/2020,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

1. The position to be taken on the Union’s behalf within the EU-Central America Association Council shall be based on the draft decision of the EU-Central America Association Council attached to this Decision.

2. Minor technical changes to the position set out in paragraph 1 may be agreed upon without a further decision of the Council.

#### Article 2

Once adopted, the Decision of the Association Council referred to in Article 1(1) shall be published in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

#### Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 9 November 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**S. HARRIS**
