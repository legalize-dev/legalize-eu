# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2399 of 20 July 2026 amending Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/117 on conservation measures in the marine protected areas of Finngrunden, Svenska Högarna, Norra Midsjöbanken and Ottenby rev in the Baltic Sea

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2013 on the Common Fisheries Policy, amending Council Regulations (EC) No 1954/2003 and (EC) No 1224/2009 and repealing Council Regulations (EC) No 2371/2002 and (EC) No 639/2004 and Council Decision 2004/585/EC [^ntr1-L_202602399EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 11(2) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Pursuant to Article 11(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013, Member States are empowered to adopt fisheries conservation measures in their waters that are necessary to comply with their obligations under Article 6 of Council Directive 92/43/EEC [^ntr2-L_202602399EN.000101-E0002] (‘Habitats Directive’), Article 4 of Directive 2009/147/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr3-L_202602399EN.000101-E0003] (‘Birds Directive’) and Article 13(4) of Directive 2008/56/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr4-L_202602399EN.000101-E0004] (‘Marine Strategy Framework Directive’).

- (2) Article 6 of Council Directive 92/43/EEC requires Member States to establish the necessary conservation measures for special areas of conservation which correspond to the ecological requirements of those natural habitat types and species protected in the sites. It also requires Member States to take appropriate steps to avoid the deterioration of natural habitats and species habitats, as well as significant disturbance of the species for which the areas have been designated.

- (3) In accordance with Article 13(4) of the Marine Strategy Framework Directive, Member States are to adopt programmes of measures to achieve or maintain good environmental status, including spatial protection measures that contribute to coherent and representative networks of marine protected areas, and adequately cover the diversity of the constituent ecosystems, such as special areas of conservation pursuant to the Habitats Directive, special protection areas pursuant to the Birds Directive and marine protected areas as agreed by the Union or Member States concerned in the framework of international or regional agreements to which they are parties.

- (4) Pursuant to Article 11(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013, where a Member State considers that measures need to be adopted to comply with the obligations under Union environmental legislation referred to in Article 11(1) of that Regulation, and other Member States have a direct management interest in the fishery to be affected by such measures, the Commission is empowered to adopt such measures by means of delegated acts upon a joint recommendation submitted by the concerned Member States.

- (5) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/117 [^ntr5-L_202602399EN.000101-E0005] establishes conservation measures for the protection of the marine environment in certain marine protected areas in the Baltic Sea.

- (6) On 5 February 2025, Sweden (the initiating Member State) together with the other Baltic Sea Member States, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland and Finland (hereinafter ‘BALTFISH’) submitted a joint recommendation to the Commission proposing to prohibit fishing in the areas of Finngrunden (consisting of three Natura 2000 sites: Norra banken SE0630263, Östra banken SE0630260 and Västra banken, SE0630262), Svenska Högarna (SE0110096), Norra Midsjöbanken, which is part of the Natura 2000 site Hoburgs bank och Midsjöbankarna (SE0330308) and Ottenby rev, which is partly a Natura 2000 site (SE0330108) and partly a nature reserve.

- (7) According to the joint recommendation, the main purpose of the measures is to ensure adequate protection of sensitive species and habitat types including the associated ecological functions in the concerned marine protected areas.

- (8) The Scientific, Technical and Economic Committee for Fisheries (STECF) reviewed the joint recommendation in the plenary of 7-11 July 2025 and concluded [^ntr6-L_202602399EN.000101-E0006] that the prohibition of all fishing in the designated areas will eliminate any fishery impacts on species and habitats within such areas, and that the prohibition of fishing would contribute to achieving the conservation objectives of each of the sites concerned by the joint recommendation. The STECF further concluded that, although no fishing was reported for the period 2015-2019, the measures would prevent potential future impacts from fishing in these sites.

- (9) Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/117 should therefore be amended accordingly.

- (10) Point 3.1 of Part A of Annex XIII to Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr7-L_202602399EN.000101-E0007] provides for mitigation measures to reduce incidental catches of sensitive species. In particular, conservation measures for the protection of the Baltic Proper harbour porpoise were introduced in that Annex by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/303 [^ntr8-L_202602399EN.000101-E0008] and later corrected by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1719 [^ntr9-L_202602399EN.000101-E0009]. Some of the measures laid down in Point 3.1 of Part A of Annex XIII to Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 concern the site Norra Midsjöbanken (Northern Midsea Bank), which is also addressed under this Regulation. The measures laid down for this site in this Regulation refer to a full fishing prohibition, and they are to replace the measures in Annex XIII to Regulation (EU) 2019/1241 that permitted fishing with pots, fish traps and longlines.

- (11) This Regulation is without prejudice to the need for additional conservation measures necessary to comply with the relevant provisions of Directives 92/43/EEC, 2009/147/EC and 2008/56/EC and the Commission’s position regarding compliance by the interested Member States with their obligations under the relevant Union environmental legislation,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/117 is amended as follows:

- (1) In Article 2, the following point (ea) is added:

> - “(ea) ‘Areas 4’ means the restricted areas that correspond to the geographical areas enclosed by sequentially joining with rhumb lines the positions listed in the Annex under the heading ‘Areas 4’ to this Regulation, which shall be measured according to the WGS84 coordinate system;”

- (2) In Article 3, the following paragraph 4 is added:

> “4.   Commercial and recreational fishing shall be prohibited in Areas 4.”

;

- (3) In Article 4, paragraph 1 is replaced by the following:

> “1.   The provisions laid down in Article 50 of Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009[^ntr*1-L_202602399EN.000101-E0010] concerning the control of fisheries restricted areas, shall apply in Areas 1, 2, 3 and 4.;

- (4) In Article 4a, paragraph 2a is replaced by the following:

> “2a.   All fishing vessels operating in Areas 3 and 4 shall be fitted with and maintain in continuous operation an AIS which meets the performance standards referred to in Article 6a of Directive 2002/59/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council[^ntr*2-L_202602399EN.000101-E0011]. The AIS may be switched off in the exceptional circumstances referred to in Article 10 of Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 and in accordance with the procedure established in that Article. Member States shall ensure that data from the AIS is made available to their competent authorities responsible for fisheries control, including cross-checks of AIS data with other available data, in accordance with Article 109 of Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009.;

> [()](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32026R2399)  Directive 2002/59/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 June 2002 establishing a Community vessel traffic monitoring and information system and repealing Council Directive 93/75/EEC ([OJ L 208, 5.8.2002, p. 10](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/JOL_2002_208_R_TOC), ELI: [http://data.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/59/oj](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/59/oj)).”

- (5) The Annex is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 20 July 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***
