# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/987 of 20 January 2020 correcting certain language versions of Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1254/2014 supplementing Directive 2010/30/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to energy labelling of residential ventilation units (Text with EEA relevance)

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10.7.2020 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 221/1 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2017/1369 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 4 July 2017 setting a framework for energy labelling and repealing Directive 2010/30/EU [^ntr1-L_2020221EN.01000101-E0001], and in particular Article 16 thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) The Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish and Swedish language versions of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1254/2014 [^ntr2-L_2020221EN.01000101-E0002] contain errors in point (e) of Article 1(2) as regards the residential ventilation units which are excluded from its scope. Those errors affect the substance of that provision.

- (2) The Maltese language version of Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1254/2014 also contains an error in point (f) of Article 1(2) as regards the residential ventilation units which are excluded from its scope. That error affects the substance of that provision.

- (3) The Swedish language version of Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1254/2014 contains an additional error in point (9) of Annex I and point (o) of Annex IV as regards one of the terms defined for the purposes of Annexes II to IX. That error affects the substance of those provisions.

- (4) The Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish and Swedish language versions of Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1254/2014 should therefore be corrected accordingly. The other language versions are not affected,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1254/2014 is corrected as follows:

- (1) Article 1(2) is corrected as follows:

- (a) point (e) is replaced by the following:

> - ‘(e) include a heat exchanger and a heat pump for heat recovery or allowing heat transfer or extraction being additional to that of the heat recovery system, except heat transfer for frost protection or defrosting;’;

- (b) *(does not concern the English language)*;

- (2) *(does not concern the English language)*;

- (3) *(does not concern the English language)*.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 20 January 2020.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
