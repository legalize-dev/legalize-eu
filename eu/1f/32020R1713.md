# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1713 of 16 November 2020 amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1382 amending certain Regulations imposing anti-dumping or anti-subsidy measures on certain steel products subject to safeguard measures

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17.11.2020 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 384/6 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2015/477 [^ntr1-L_2020384EN.01000601-E0001] of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2015, on measures that the Union may take in relation to the combined effect of anti-dumping or anti-subsidy measures with safeguard measures,

Whereas:

- (1) By Regulation (EU) 2019/159 [^ntr2-L_2020384EN.01000601-E0002], the Commission imposed safeguard measures with respect to certain steel products for a period of three years. Those measures take the form of tariff-rate quotas applicable for specified periods, by which an above-quota tariff duty of 25 % is payable when imports exceed a specified threshold corresponding to the average level of imports in the years 2015 to 2017.

- (2) By Regulation (EU) 2019/1382 [^ntr3-L_2020384EN.01000601-E0003], the Commission put in place a mechanism preventing the concurrent application of this above-quota tariff duty with anti-dumping and/or countervailing measures also applicable to some of the steel products.

- (3) For this purpose, the annex to Regulation (EU) 2019/1382 listed all anti-dumping and anti-subsidy measures on products also subject to the safeguard measures at the time, and specified the anti-dumping and/or anti-subsidy duty that should apply once the above-quota tariff becomes applicable.

- (4) On 6 October 2020, by Regulation (EU) 2020/1408 [^ntr4-L_2020384EN.01000601-E0004], the Commission imposed definitive anti-dumping measures on imports of certain hot rolled stainless steel sheets and coils originating in Indonesia, the People’s Republic of China and Taiwan.

- (5) As stated in recital 310 of Regulation (EU) 2020/1408, the mechanism preventing the concurrent application of measures applies to these products since they are also subject to safeguard measures (product number 8). The annex to Regulation (EU) 2019/1382 should therefore be amended in order to include Regulation (EU) 2020/1408.

- (6) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Trade Defence Instruments Committee,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1382 is amended as follows:

- (1) in Annex 1.B ‘List of the regulations imposing anti-dumping and anti-subsidy measures on the products that are subject to the safeguard measure’, the following paragraph is added:

> ‘19)   COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2020/1408 of 6 October 2020 imposing a definitive anti-dumping duty and definitively collecting the provisional duty imposed on imports of certain hot rolled stainless steel sheets and coils originating in Indonesia, the People’s Republic of China and Taiwan, [OJ L 325, 7.10.2020, p. 26](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/JOL_2020_325_R_TOC).’;

- (2) in Annex 2 ‘Rates of anti-dumping and/or countervailing duties applicable as long as a safeguard duty is due on the same product’, the following Annex 2.18 is added:

> ‘ANNEX 2.18

> Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1408 of 6 October 2020 imposing a definitive anti-dumping duty and definitively collecting the provisional duty imposed on imports of certain hot rolled stainless steel sheets and coils originating in Indonesia, the People’s Republic of China and Taiwan, [OJ L 325, 7.10.2020, p. 26](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/JOL_2020_325_R_TOC).
