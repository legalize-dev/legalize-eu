# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2267 of 13 October 2026 establishing the format and frequency of data and the list of relevant raw materials to be reported by the Member States for the purposes of reporting on environmental data on industrial installations under Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/1741 (notified under document C(2026) 7610)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 April 2024 on reporting of environmental data from industrial installations, establishing an Industrial Emissions Portal and repealing Regulation (EC) No 166/2006 [^ntr1-L_202602267EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 6(1), second subparagraph, and Article 7(1) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Reporting of key environmental pressures generated by industrial installations is essential in order to enhance public access to information, thereby facilitating public participation in environmental decision-making as well as identifying sources of industrial pollution, and to enable industrial pollution to be monitored in order to contribute to its prevention and reduction. With this aim, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, an electronic and publicly available Industrial Emissions Portal (‘Portal’) is established.

- (2) In accordance with Article 7(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, and in order to ensure consistency and coherence of data provided by the Member States and to facilitate the exchange of information between the Member States and the Commission, the Commission establishes a format for this purpose.

- (3) Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/1741 [^ntr2-L_202602267EN.000101-E0002] establishes the format and frequency of data to be made available by Member States for the purposes of reporting in accordance with Article 7(2) of Regulation (EC) No 166/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr3-L_202602267EN.000101-E0003]. Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 repeals and replaces Regulation (EC) No 166/2006, since that Regulation needed to be substantially modified for reasons of regulatory effectiveness, legal certainty, clarity and transparency. Those modifications reflect the need to streamline reporting obligations by enhancing synergies with other Union environmental law on pollution from industrial installations and to provide additional contextual information. It is therefore necessary to replace the reporting format established by Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/1741 with a format adapted to the reporting obligations laid down in Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 and to repeal Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/1741.

- (4) Pursuant to Article 6(1), second subparagraph, the Commission establishes a list of relevant raw materials to be reported, which should be based on the best available techniques reference documents drawn up pursuant to Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr4-L_202602267EN.000101-E0004]. The list of relevant raw materials should be limited to materials for which reporting is meaningful, feasible and relevant for assessing the environmental performance of the activities concerned. For water and energy, the units and formats should ensure comparable reporting across installations and Member States, taking account of the nature of the activities concerned.

- (5) To ensure efficient reporting in the format established by this Decision and to reduce the administrative burden for Member States, the electronic reporting templates containing fields set out in Annex I to this Decision will be prefilled, from the second reporting year, on the basis of the information previously reported or validated by Member States. Since a large part of the required administrative and identification information is stable over time, such pre-filling should facilitate reporting while preserving the responsibility of the Member States to verify, update where necessary and submit the information required under this Decision.

- (6) To further simplify reporting and reduce the administrative burden to Member States, the reporting format should allow the River Basin District corresponding to each installation to be derived from the geographical coordinates reported for that installation and from the most recent valid River Basin District codes in the Water Information System for Europe (WISE).

- (7) As all industrial sectors covered by Directive 2010/75/EU fall within the scope of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, and pursuant to Article 4(1), point (c), and recitals 6 and 8 of that Regulation, the Portal should incorporate the information reported under Directive 2010/75/EU, in particular information reported under Article 72 of that Directive. Since reporting under this Decision and under Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135 [^ntr5-L_202602267EN.000101-E0005] is carried out through the same electronic reporting tool, and to avoid double reporting and unnecessary administrative burden, the administrative information set out in Annex I to this Decision should be reported only where that information has not already been reported in accordance with Directive 2010/75/EU.

- (8) Since administrative information represent a core element of the data architecture of the Portal and reporting obligations on installations falling under the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 and Directive 2010/75/EU, and to ensure alignment with the reporting deadline in accordance with Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135, it is appropriate to set an earlier date for reporting or validating this administrative information by Member States, as compared to thematic information.

- (9) To ensure proportionate and efficient reporting obligations for both Member States and operators, and that the information made available through the Portal is complete, interpretable and comparable, it is necessary to specify how Member States are to report cases where releases of pollutants to air, water or land, off-site transfers of pollutants in waste water or off-site transfers of waste do not exceed the applicable reporting thresholds laid down in Article 6(1), points (b) and (c), and Annex II to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244. For the first reporting year in which this Decision applies, Member States should report, together with the administrative data set out in Annex I to this Decision, whether those releases or transfers are below the applicable thresholds. For subsequent reporting years, Member States may either report that status every year or report it only the first time it is observed. Where national reporting systems require operators to report release or transfer values irrespective of whether the applicable thresholds are exceeded, values reported below those thresholds should be regarded as equivalent to a declaration that the releases or transfers are below the applicable thresholds, without requiring a separate declaration.

- (10) In order to ensure coherent implementation of the new reporting obligations in a timely manner, Member States should have sufficient time to adapt their national reporting frameworks. Member States should therefore be required to report information first for the reporting year 2027. Since reporting on the use of relevant raw materials requires specific adaptations to those frameworks and the collection of new information by operators, it is appropriate to provide for a later first reporting year for that information.

- (11) Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 provides for reporting at installation level, aiming to ensure synergies between the Portal and databases on environmental pressures from industrial installations and to ensure coherence with and support the implementation of Directive 2010/75/EU. Following this aim, it is necessary for installations rearing poultry and pigs that the timeline for which reporting is required is in alignment with the dates from which reporting obligations apply to those installations under the implementing measures adopted pursuant to Directive 2010/75/EU. This Decision therefore provides for a phased application of the reporting obligations for those installations. Installations rearing poultry and pigs which were already included within the scope of Regulation (EC) No 166/2006 on 1 May 2024 and Directive 2010/75/EU on 3 August 2024, will report from the reporting year 2027.

- (12) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 17 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

##### Administrative information

1. For the purpose of reporting administrative information to the Commission pursuant to Article 7(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall use the format set out in Annex I to this Decision and the harmonised electronic reporting tool made available by the Commission.

2. The administrative information set out in Annex I shall be submitted to the Commission by 30 September of the year following the reporting year.

3. For installations falling within the scope of Directive 2010/75/EU, Member States shall report the information set out in Annex I only to the extent that the same information has not already been reported in accordance with Article 72 of that Directive.

#### Article 2

##### Thematic information

1. For the purpose of reporting thematic information to the Commission, pursuant to Article 7(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall use the format set out in Annex II to this Decision and the harmonised electronic reporting tool made available by the Commission.

2. The thematic information set out in Annexes II, IV, V, VI and VII shall be submitted to the Commission by 30 November of the year following the reporting year.

#### Article 3

##### Nomenclature of activities

For the purpose of reporting the activities listed in Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall use the nomenclature set out in Annex III.

#### Article 4

##### First reporting years

1. The information set out in Annexes I, II, IV, VI and VII shall be first submitted for the reporting year 2027.

2. The information set out in Annex V shall be first submitted for the reporting year 2028.

3. By way of derogation from paragraph 1, for installations rearing poultry and pigs which were not included within the scope of Directive 2010/75/EU in the version in force on 3 August 2024, the first reporting year shall be:

- (a) 2030 for installations with a capacity of 600 livestock units (LSU) or more;

- (b) 2031 for installations with a capacity of 400 livestock units (LSU) or more;

- (c) 2032 for all other installations rearing poultry and pigs within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 set out in Annex I to that Regulation.

#### Article 5

##### Prefilled templates

1. From the reporting year 2028, the Commission assisted by the European Environment Agency, shall make available to the Member States prefilled electronic reporting templates for the information set out in Annex I, through the harmonised electronic reporting tool. Those templates shall be based on the data reported by Member States for the reporting year 2027. In subsequent years, the prefilling will be based on the most recent information validated or otherwise reported by Member States. From the reporting year 2028, Member States may choose one of the following methods:

- (a) to verify and, if necessary, update the prefilled templates and submit them to the Commission;

- (b) to complete the templates without using the prefilled version.

2. From the reporting year 2027, the Commission assisted by the European Environment Agency, shall allocate each installation to a River Basin District based on the reported coordinates of the installation and the most recent valid River Basin District codes in the Water Information System for Europe (‘WISE’).

#### Article 6

##### Below-threshold reporting and off-site transfers of waste

1. In accordance with Article 6(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, where any of the thresholds set out in Article 6(1), points (a), (b) or (c), of that Regulation are not exceeded for any installation in their territory, Member States shall, in relation to those installations, in addition to administrative data set out in Annex I, report that the release of pollutants or the off-site transfers of pollutants or waste are below the applicable reporting thresholds for the first reporting year.

2. Member States may decide to report on the below-threshold status every year or to report it only the first time the below-threshold status is observed.

3. In Member States where the national reporting system does not prescribe reporting thresholds for operators to fulfil the requirements of Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, any reporting of release or transfer values that are below the thresholds set out in Annex II to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 or Article 6(1), point (b) of that Regulation, shall be regarded as equivalent to a declaration that the releases or transfers are below the applicable thresholds for the purposes of Article 6(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244. In such cases, operators shall not be required to submit a separate declaration of below-threshold status to their competent authority, as the information provided under the national system is deemed to fulfil the requirement of Article 6(2) of that Regulation.

4. For the purposes of reporting under Article 6(1), point (b), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall ensure that operators report off-site transfers of waste only when such waste leaves the boundaries of the facility.

#### Article 7

##### Confidentiality

In accordance with Article 11 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall indicate for each facility, installation and part of an installation, which data cannot be made public and provide the reasons for that. In such cases, only the specific data elements identified as confidential by the Member State, will not be made public through the Industrial Emissions Portal. This shall not affect the publication of other data for the same facility, installation or a part of an installation.

#### Article 8

##### Repeal

Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/1741 is repealed with effect from 31 December 2027.

#### Article 9

##### Application

This Decision shall apply from 1 January 2028.

#### Article 10

##### Addressees

This Decision is addressed to the Member States.

Done at Brussels, 13 October 2026.

## ANNEX IV

Establishing the units and metrics to be reported by Member States under field 9.1 “Production Volume” (Annex II of this implementing act)

### 1. **Definitions**

For the purposes of this Annex, the following definitions shall apply:

- 1. ‘tonnes of the surface treatment substances in ready to use state’ means the amount of active substance in a ready to use state;

- 2. ‘tonnes of products’ means, unless otherwise stated, the weight of the stated metric, including any inherent moisture content of the products, but excluding any product packaging or containment;

- 3. ‘tonnes of extracted material’ means, the weight of ore extracted from the deposit for the purpose of further processing, beneficiation or upgrading, including any inherent moisture content of the ore at the point of extraction or delivery to the on-site treatment process. It excludes overburden, waste rock and other excavated materials not intended for further processing, as well as any material that is directly discarded without being classified as ore;

- 4. ‘tonnes of products as oil equivalents’ means the output of an installation expressed as the amount of energy released by burning 1 tonne of crude oil, considering that the energy content of a tonne of crude oil is 42 gigajoules;

- 5. ‘gigajoules of useful energy output’ means the energy effectively converted into electricity or heat expressed in gigajoules and served to the grid or the final user. In case of MCPs, where the operator can demonstrate to the competent authority that useful energy output is not served to the grid or the final user, the operator shall report energy output (GJ) that is not marketed but consumed onsite;

- 6. ‘tonnes of incoming waste’ means the weight of all incoming waste, within a calendar year, which are recovered or disposed of at the installation subject to the reporting. For activities A-5.5 and A-5.6, means the weight of all incoming waste, within a calendar year, to an installation for its temporary or underground storage, respectively;

- 7. ‘tonnes of battery cells’ means the total mass, expressed in tonnes, of battery cells produced in an installation during the reporting calendar year, including cathodes, anodes, electrolytes, separators and assembled cells, where such components are manufactured as part of the battery cell production process. The quantity reported shall cover all viable outputs that are sold, stored on-site or used on-site for further processing and shall refer to production before assembly into battery modules or packs. Outputs that are spoiled, rejected or off-specification shall be excluded;

- 8. ‘tonnes of hydrogen’ means the total mass, expressed in tonnes, of hydrogen generated during the reporting calendar year. The quantity reported shall refer to the total amount of hydrogen obtained as a product, material or substance intended for use or for placing on the market, including hydrogen that is stored onsite;

- 9. ‘cubic metres of treated waste-water’ means the volume of water treated during the reporting year by the relevant waste-water treatment installation;

- 10. ‘tonnes of recovered phosphorus’ means the total mass, expressed in tonnes of elemental phosphorus (P), recovered during the calendar year from wastewater, sewage sludge, sludge ash or other residual streams generated by the treatment of wastewater. The quantity reported shall refer to the total phosphorus content in the recovered material, expressed as elemental phosphorus (P), where such recovery is carried out and the quantity is attributable to the installation, irrespective of the subsequent use, storage or destination of the recovered material;

- 11. ’number of livestock units (‘LSU’)’ means livestock units as defined in Art 3(23b) of Directive 2010/75/EU.

### 2. **General rules**

- (a) Unless otherwise stated, the data on production volume shall cover total viable outputs from an installation and shall include the sum of all outputs during the year that are sold, stored on-site, and used on-site for further processing. The sum of outputs that are spoiled, rejected or off-specification shall be excluded.

- (b) The production volume field 9.1 of Annex II has a multiplicity of “one to many”. The reporting on production volume shall cover activities performed in the installation according to Annex I point 3.4.

- (c) Where possible, the data on production volume shall be reported in accordance with the Prodcom methodology [^ntr1-L_202602267EN.002001-E0001] for national statistics on the production of manufactured goods and with the methodology laid down in Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 of the European Parliament and of the Council.

- (d) Unless otherwise stated, the reported production volume shall exclude the weight of any packaging, containers or other containment materials used for the storage, transport or sale of products.

- (e) The following data shall be reported under field 9.1 of Annex II:

- (i). quantity expressed in the unit/metric set out in point 3;

- (ii). comments (optional).

## ANNEX V

Establishing the list of relevant raw materials to be reported under Article 6(1)(d) of Regulation 2024/1244, as well as their units, as per field 10.1 “Relevant Raw materials” [^ntr1-L_202602267EN.003001-E0001] (Annex II of this implementing act)

### 1. **Definitions**

For the purposes of this Annex, the following definitions shall apply:

- (1) ‘Secondary raw material’ means a material which originates from outside the installation and which can be used as input to an activity replacing the virgin raw material, where the material is:

- a. the result of a recycling or other recovery operation within the meaning of Article 3 of Directive 2008/98/EC or

- b. a by-product, meaning a material resulting from a production process the primary aim of which is not the production of that material, and that meets the conditions laid down in Article 5 of Directive 2008/98/EC.

- (2) Blast furnace slags’ means the non-metallic co-product generated during the production of hot metal in a blast furnace, consisting predominantly of gangue from the iron ore combined with added fluxes (e.g. limestone or dolomite) and fuel ash, which forms a distinct molten phase separate from the iron and is subsequently tapped and cooled or granulated, as described for blast furnace processes in the Best available Technique Reference document (BREF) for the iron and steel sector.

- (3) ‘Cullet’ means externally sourced glass generated from the recovery of waste glass used as input to the glass-melting process, regardless of its origin or previous use, including, inter alia, container glass, flat glass and automotive (vehicle) glass, that is processed for remelting during the reporting year.

- (4) ‘Paper for recycling’ means secondary paper and board, including paper- or board-based products, originating from outside the installation that are supplied for use as a secondary fibre raw material in the production of paper, board or other pulp- and paper-based products, and which have been separately collected or otherwise diverted from the waste streams after use. It includes printed or unprinted paper and board, converting residues, and fibres recovered from commercial or post-consumer sources, provided they originate from outside the installation.

- (5) ‘Recovered wood’ means secondary wood materials or wood-based products, originating from outside the installation, supplied for use as secondary wood raw material in the manufacture of wood-based panels or other wood products and which arise from wood discarded after use or wood residues generated in industrial processes. It includes post-consumer wood, pre-consumer wood residues, offcuts and wood packaging, provided they meet the technical specifications required for the production process.

- (6) ‘Used oil’ refers to waste oils, as defined in Article 3(3) of Directive 2008/98/EC, that are used as feedstock substituting primary crude fractions and re-refined.

- (7) ‘Recycled polymers’ means polymers used as raw-material inputs in the production of polymers that replace virgin polymer feedstock, originate from outside the installation, and are obtained from plastic waste through a recycling operation within the meaning of Directive 2008/98/EC, including mechanical recycling and chemical recycling. Chemically recycled content shall only be reported where supported by supplier certification or mass balance documentation. Recycled polymers shall not include:

- a. polymers arising from internal re-use of materials within the same installation,

- b. by-products that have not been obtained from waste through recycling; or

- c. materials used for energy recovery.

### 2. **General rules**

- (1) Reporting of raw materials shall be carried out on the basis of the main activity, as set out in Section 3 of Annex I to this implementing act. “Units and metrics” are defined below. Once the relevant reportable fields have been identified in accordance with the relevant main activity, reporting shall cover the entire installation.

- (2) Operators shall not apply any reporting threshold to the reporting of raw materials under this Annex.

## ANNEX VI

Establishing the units and metrics to be reported by Member States under field 10.2 “Energy use” (Annex II to this implementing act)

Definitions:

- (1) “Waste” in “waste used as fuel” – means waste as defined in Article 3(37) of Directive 2010/75/EU and “fuel” as defined in Art 3(24) of the same Directive.

### 1. **General rules**

- a) Operators shall report on energy use for all activities performed in the installation in the scope of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 – not applicable to B-1, B-2, B-3 and G-1 activities.

- b) The energy use field 10.2 of Annex II has a multiplicity of “one to many”.

### 2. **Units and metrics**

Fuel use onsite to produce energy or heat as well as renewable energy production onsite and electricity or heat imported for the IEPR-related activities:

- — Coal, lignite, biomass, peat, other solid fuels (specifying the type), liquid fuels, natural gas, other gases (specifying the type) in TJ energy input;

- — Waste used as fuel (specifying type) (TJ energy input);

- — Renewable energy produced onsite (TJ energy input);

- — Electricity from the grid (TJ energy input);

- — Heat provided or received from an external provider (e.g. district heating) (TJ energy input).

## ANNEX VII

Establishing the units and metrics to be reported by Member States under field 10.3 “Water use” (Annex II to this implementing act):

“Water use” means water abstracted from water bodies or obtained from the public water supply network which is used by the installation during a reporting year.

### 1. **General rules**

- a) Operators shall report the quantity of water use related to activities in the scope of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 performed at the installation during the reporting year – not applicable to B-1, B-2, B-3 and G-1 activities.

- b) The water use field 10.3 of Annex II has a multiplicity of “one to one”.

### 2. **Units and metrics**

This reporting refers to the amount of water consumed by the installation in m<sup>3</sup>.
