# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2544 of 10 November 2026 on the clearance of accounts of the paying agencies of Member States concerning expenditure financed by the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD) in the last execution year of the EAFRD 2014–2022 programming period (16 October 2024–31 December 2025) (notified under document C(2026) 7928)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2021/2116 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 2 December 2021 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 [^ntr1-L_202602544EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 104(1), second subparagraph, point (a)(iv), thereof,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2020/2220 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 December 2020 laying down certain transitional provisions for support from the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD) and from the European Agricultural Guarantee Fund (EAGF) in the years 2021 and 2022 and amending Regulations (EU) No 1305/2013, (EU) No 1306/2013 and (EU) No 1307/2013 as regards resources and application in the years 2021 and 2022 and Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 as regards resources and the distribution of such support in respect of the years 2021 and 2022 [^ntr2-L_202602544EN.000101-E0002],

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 352/78, (EC) No 165/94, (EC) No 2799/98, (EC) No 814/2000, (EC) No 1290/2005 and (EC) No 485/2008 [^ntr3-L_202602544EN.000101-E0003], and in particular Article 51 thereof,

After consulting the Committee on the Agricultural Funds,

Whereas:

- (1) Articles 1 and 2 of Regulation (EU) 2020/2220 extend the period of duration of programmes supported by the EAFRD and the period from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2020 for the application of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr4-L_202602544EN.000101-E0004] until 31 December 2022.

- (2) Pursuant to Article 104(1), second subparagraph, point (a), of Regulation (EU) 2021/2116, Article 4(1), point (b), Article 5, Article 7(3), Articles 9, 17, 21 and 34, Article 35(4), Articles 36, 37, 38, 40 to 43, 51, 52, 54, 56, 59, 63, 64, 67, 68, 70 to 75, 77, 91 to 97, 99 and 100, Article 102(2) and Articles 110 and 111 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 continue to apply to EAFRD, including for the last execution year of the EAFRD 2014–2022 programming period (16 October 2024–31 December 2025).

- (3) Pursuant to Article 64, second paragraph, point (a), of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/128 [^ntr5-L_202602544EN.000101-E0005], Article 2, Article 3(1), first subparagraph, Article 3(2), Article 4(1), point (b), Article 5, Article 6, Article 7, Articles 21 to 25, Article 27, Article 28, Article 29, Article 30(1), points (a), (b) and (c), Article 30(2), (3) and (4), and Articles 31 to 40 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 908/2014 [^ntr6-L_202602544EN.000101-E0006] continue to apply, as regards the EAFRD, in relation to expenditure incurred by the beneficiaries and payments made by the paying agency in the framework of the implementation of rural development programmes pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr7-L_202602544EN.000101-E0007], including for the last execution year of the EAFRD 2014–2022 programming period (16 October 2024–31 December 2025).

- (4) Pursuant to Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, the Commission, on the basis of the annual accounts submitted by the Member States, accompanied by the information required for the clearance of accounts and an audit opinion regarding the completeness, accuracy and veracity of the accounts and the reports established by the certification bodies, is to clear the accounts of the paying agencies referred to in Article 7 of that Regulation.

- (5) In accordance with Article 37(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, after receiving the last annual progress report on the implementation of a rural development programme, the Commission is to pay the balance, subject to the availability of resources, on the basis of the financial plan in force, the annual accounts for the last execution year for the relevant rural development programme and of the corresponding clearance decision.

- (6) In accordance with Article 37(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, Article 65(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013 and Article 2 of Regulation (EU) 2020/2220, the annual accounts for the last execution year, are to be presented to the Commission no later than six months after the final eligibility date of expenditure, which was extended to 31 December 2025, and are to cover the expenditure effected by the paying agency up to that date.

- (7) Pursuant to Article 59(4), point (e), of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013, operations financed from amounts transferred to the EAFRD pursuant to Article 136a(1) of Council Regulation (EC) No 73/2009 [^ntr8-L_202602544EN.000101-E0008] and Article 7(2) and Article 14(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1307/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr9-L_202602544EN.000101-E0009] may benefit from a EAFRD contribution rate of up to 100 %. That derogation is to apply only within the limits of the amounts transferred from the EAGF, as set out in the financing plan of the rural development programme of the Member State approved by the Commission. Where a Member State sets such EAFRD contribution rate higher than the contribution rate set out in Article 59(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013 and the cumulative expenditure declared by a Member State at that rate exceeds the transferred amount provided for in the financing plan, the excess is not eligible for Union financing at a rate of 100 %, but is subject to the co-financing rate applicable pursuant to Article 59(3) of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013. Any amount reimbursed in excess is therefore to be deducted from the final balance in the clearance of the annual accounts.

- (8) Payment of advances were allowed pursuant to Article 42(2) and Article 45(4) of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013. According to Article 63 of that Regulation, the advance is to be secured with a bank guarantee or facility provided by a public authority and may be released when the amount of the actual expenditure corresponding to the public contribution related to the operation exceeds the amount of the advance. Advances paid to beneficiaries and related to completed projects under the 2014–2022 programming period as extended by Regulation (EU) 2020/2220, are to be settled by the 31 December 2025, unless those advances were linked to ongoing projects for which the financing will be ensured under the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) Strategic Plan 2023–2027, pursuant to Article 155(4) of Regulation (EU) 2021/2115 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr10-L_202602544EN.000101-E0010].

- (9) Article 36(3), first subparagraph, point (b), of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 provides that interim payments are to be made by the Commission without overrun of the total programmed EAFRD contribution. Pursuant to Article 23(2) of Implementing Regulation (EU) No 908/2014, where the combined total of declarations of expenditure exceeds the total programmed contribution for a rural development programme, the amount to be paid is to be capped at the programmed amount, without prejudice to the ceiling provided for in Article 34(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013.

- (10) In accordance with Article 42 of Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013, the eligible expenditure of the financial instrument is to be the total amount of programme contributions effectively paid or, in the case of guarantees, committed by the financial instrument within the eligibility period, corresponding to: payments to final recipients, and in the cases referred to in Article 37(7) of that Regulation payments to the benefit of final recipients, resources committed for guarantee contracts, capitalised interest rate subsidies or guarantee fee subsidies and to reimbursement of management costs incurred or payment of management fees of the financial instrument. Programme contributions not meeting these conditions, are not eligible for Union financing.

- (11) In accordance with Article 75(1), fourth subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, the rules on payment deadlines for measures under rural development apply in the context of the integrated administration and control system referred to in Article 67(2) of that Regulation. The reductions for non-compliance with the payment deadlines, calculated in accordance with Article 5a of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 907/2014 [^ntr11-L_202602544EN.000101-E0011], follow the rules laid down in Articles 40 and 41 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 and are to be taken into account in the clearance decision for the last execution year. Those reductions may be examined, where appropriate, under conformity clearance procedure pursuant to Article 52 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013.

- (12) This Decision should also cover the additional resources referred to in Article 58a of Regulation (EU) No 1305/2013.

- (13) Pursuant to Article 34(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, the combined total of prefinancing and interim payments are not to exceed 95 % of the EAFRD’s contribution to each rural development programme. The outstanding balances are to be settled at the closure of the programme.

- (14) The Member States submitted to the Commission a complete set of accounts by 30 June 2026. The annual accounts and the accompanying documents permit the Commission to take a decision on the completeness, accuracy and veracity of the annual accounts submitted.

- (15) In accordance with Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, this Decision is without prejudice to the decisions the Commission may take subsequently pursuant to Article 52 of that Regulation to exclude from Union financing expenditure not effected in accordance with Union rules.

- (16) In adopting this Decision, the Commission should deduct the amounts reduced or suspended on the basis of Article 41 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013, in order to avoid making any undue or untimely payments, or reimbursing amounts that could later be subject to financial correction.

- (17) The Commission has checked the information submitted by the Member States and has communicated the results of its checks to the Member States by 30 September 2026, along with the necessary amendments,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The accounts of the Member States’ paying agencies concerning expenditure financed by the European Agricultural Fund for Rural Development (EAFRD) in the last execution year of the EAFRD 2014–2022 programming period (16 October 2024–31 December 2025) are hereby cleared.

The amounts recoverable from, or payable to, each Member State under each rural development programme pursuant to this Decision, are set out in Annex I to this Decision.

#### Article 2

The reductions for non-compliance with the payment deadlines in accordance with Article 75(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 under each Member State rural development programme are set out in Annex II to this Decision.

#### Article 3

The final balances for the 2014–2022 rural development programmes for which all relevant annual accounts of the Member States’ paying agencies have been cleared are set out in Annex III to this Decision.

#### Article 4

This Decision is without prejudice to future conformity clearance decisions that the Commission may take pursuant to Article 52 of Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 to exclude from Union financing expenditure not effected in accordance with Union rules.

#### Article 5

This Decision is addressed to the Member States.

Done at Brussels, 10 November 2026.

***For the Commission***

**Christophe HANSEN**

***Member of the Commission***
