# Council Decision (EU) 2026/2557 of 9 November 2026 on the position to be taken on behalf of the European Union within the Customs Cooperation Committee established under the Agreement establishing an Association between the European Community and Turkey concerning the adoption of a decision laying down detailed rules for the application of Decision No 1/95 of the EC-Turkey Association Council

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 207(4), first subparagraph, in conjunction with Article 218(9), thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the European Commission,

Whereas:

- (1) The Agreement establishing an Association between the European Community and Turkey [^ntr1-L_202602557EN.000101-E0001] (the ‘Association Agreement’) was concluded by the Union by means of Council Decision 64/732/EEC [^ntr2-L_202602557EN.000101-E0002]. The rules concerning the final phase of the EU-Türkiye Customs Union are laid down in Decision No 1/95 of the EC-Turkey Association Council [^ntr3-L_202602557EN.000101-E0003] of 22 December 1995 (‘Decision No 1/95’).

- (2) Decision No 2/69 of the Association Council [^ntr4-L_202602557EN.000101-E0004] of 15 December 1969 established the Customs Cooperation Committee pursuant to Article 24 of the Association Agreement.

- (3) Pursuant to Article 28 of Decision No 1/95, the Customs Cooperation Committee is to lay down the appropriate measures to implement the customs provisions set out in that Decision. For that purpose, the Customs Cooperation Committee adopted Decision No 1/2006 [^ntr5-L_202602557EN.000101-E0005] of 26 September 2006 .

- (4) The Customs Cooperation Committee, during its next meeting or by Exchange of Letters, is to adopt a decision regarding detailed rules for the application of Decision No 1/95.

- (5) The decision of the Customs Cooperation Committee will, if adopted, produce legal effects for the Union.

- (6) The Joint Committee of the Regional Convention on Pan-Euro-Mediterranean Preferential Rules of Origin (the ‘PEM Convention’) adopted Decision No 1/2023 of 7 December 2023 on the Amendment of the Regional Convention on pan-Euro-Mediterranean preferential rules of origin [^ntr6-L_202602557EN.000101-E0006], which entered into force on 1 January 2025.

- (7) It is necessary to align the provisions of Decision No 1/2006 of the Customs Cooperation Committee with Decision No 1/2023 and to bring them in line with the new provisions of the EU’s and Türkiye’s customs legislation. It is also necessary to insert the text of various endorsements in the new official languages of the Union resulting from the enlargement of the Union.

- (8) Transitional provisions maintained the application of the 2012 Rules of Origin of the PEM Convention [^ntr7-L_202602557EN.000101-E0007] between 1 January and 31 December 2025 in parallel with the revised rules of the PEM Convention as amended by Decision No 2/2024 [^ntr8-L_202602557EN.000101-E0008] of the Joint Committee of the PEM Convention.

- (9) Following the expiry of the transitional provisions established under Decision 2/2024 of the Joint Committee of the PEM Convention on 31 December 2025, certain provisions of the envisaged Decision of the Customs Cooperation Committee should apply retroactively from 1 January 2026 in the general interest to ensure the uninterrupted operation of the Customs Union and the continuity of diagonal cumulation under the revised PEM Convention. That retroactive application should allow transactions carried out as of 1 January 2026 to benefit from the adapted non-alteration requirements, including storage and splitting of consignments under customs supervision in third countries, the extended period for presenting A.TR movement certificates, and the procedures for issuing duplicates and certificates retrospectively. The recognition of proofs of Union or Turkish origin issued in common preferential partner countries, together with the requirements governing supplier’s declarations, should ensure the continuity of the documentary evidence supporting diagonal cumulation, including where working or processing is taken into account through full cumulation. The corresponding administrative cooperation and record-keeping provisions should ensure the verification of that evidence. Such retroactive application safeguards the legitimate expectations of economic operators by ensuring continuity in the use of the documentary evidence supporting free circulation and preferential treatment for transactions carried out since 1 January 2026.

- (10) It is recalled that Türkiye is under an obligation to ensure the full and non-discriminatory implementation of the Additional Protocol to the Association Agreement towards all Member States, including the Republic of Cyprus, and to apply the Customs Union effectively and without discrimination to all Member States.

- (11) It is therefore appropriate to establish the position to be taken on the Union’s behalf within the Customs Cooperation Committee with regard to the adoption of a Decision laying down detailed rules for the application of Decision No 1/95 of the EC-Turkey Association Council,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

The position to be taken on the Union’s behalf within the EU–Türkiye Customs Cooperation Committee under the Association Agreement shall be based on the draft decision of the Customs Cooperation Committee attached to this Decision.

#### Article 2

Minor technical changes to the position set out in the Annex may be agreed upon without a further decision of the Council.

#### Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 9 November 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**S. HARRIS**
