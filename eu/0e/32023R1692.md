# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1692 of 30 August 2023 approving non-minor amendments to the product specification for the protected geographical indication (‘Fränkischer Karpfen / Frankenkarpfen / Karpfen aus Franken’)

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6.9.2023 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 219/8 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 November 2012 on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_2023219EN.01000801-E0001], and in particular Article 52(2) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) In accordance with Article 5(2) of Regulation (EU) 2021/2117 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr2-L_2023219EN.01000801-E0002], the rules applicable before 7 December 2021 continue to apply to applications for approval of amendments to a product specification received by the Commission pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 before 8 June 2022.

- (2) On 7 February 2022 the Commission received from Germany an application for the approval of amendments to the product specification for the protected geographical indication ‘Fränkischer Karpfen / Frankenkarpfen / Karpfen aus Franken’ registered under Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1007/2012 [^ntr3-L_2023219EN.01000801-E0003].

- (3) The Commission examined Germany’s application in accordance with Article 50(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 in conjunction with the first subparagraph of Article 53(1) of that Regulation, as applicable before 7 December 2021.

- (4) Since the amendments in question are not minor within the meaning of Article 53(2) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012, as applicable before 7 December 2021, the Commission published the amendment application in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr4-L_2023219EN.01000801-E0004] as required by Article 50(2), point (a), of that Regulation.

- (5) As no reasoned statement of opposition under Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 has been received by the Commission, the amendments to the product specification should be approved,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The amendments to the product specification published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* regarding the protected geographical indication ‘Fränkischer Karpfen / Frankenkarpfen / Karpfen aus Franken’ are hereby approved.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 30 August 2023.

***For the Commission,***

***On behalf of the President,***

**Janusz WOJCIECHOWSKI**

***Member of the Commission***
