THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community, in particular Article 101, paragraph 3, thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) A Delegation of the European Union already exists in Canada.

- (2) In accordance with Article 5(6) of Council Decision 2010/427/EU [^ntr1-L_202602521EN.000101-E0001], the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy (‘High Representative’) has negotiated an Agreement with the Government of Canada, as host country, on the Establishment and the Privileges and Immunities of the Delegation of the European Union in Canada.

- (3) The scope of the Agreement on the Establishment and the Privileges and Immunities of the Delegation of the European Union in Canada covers the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community.

- (4) Therefore, the Agreement on the Establishment and the Privileges and Immunities of the Delegation of the European Union in Canada should be also approved on behalf of the European Atomic Energy Community,

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

#### Article 1

The Agreement between the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community of the one part, and the Government of Canada, of the other part, on the Establishment and the Privileges and Immunities of the Delegation of the European Union in Canada (‘the Agreement’) is hereby approved on behalf of the European Atomic Energy Community. The text of the Agreement is attached to this Decision.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 23 October 2026.

***For the Commission***

**Dan JØRGENSEN**

***Member of the Commission***

[^ntr1-L_202602521EN.000101-E0001]: Council Decision 2010/427/EU of 26 July 2010 establishing the organisation and functioning of the European External Action Service ([OJ L 201, 3.8.2010, p. 30](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/JOL_2010_201_R_TOC), ELI: [http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/2010/427/oj](http://data.europa.eu/eli/dec/2010/427/oj)).

The Government of Canada, on the one part, and the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community, on the other part (the “Parties”),

Desirous of further strengthening and developing the friendly relations and cooperation between them;

Wishing to lay down terms concerning the privileges and immunities of the Delegation of the European Union in Canada;

Desirous to accord to the European Union the same treatment as accorded to diplomatic missions established in Canada;

Have agreed as follows:

#### **ARTICLE 1**

##### Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

- a. “Agreement” means this agreement;

- b. “Delegation of the European Union in Canada” (also referred to as “the Delegation of the European Union” or “the Delegation”) includes the Delegation of the European Union in Ottawa, Ontario, the European Union Office in Montréal, Quebec, and any future offices of the European Union that are established on the territory of Canada with the express consent of Canada;

- c. "Permanent resident of Canada" means a person admitted to Canada for permanent residence in accordance with applicable Canadian immigration law;

- d. “Convention” means the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations of 18 April 1961.

#### **ARTICLE 2**

##### Establishment of the Delegation of the European Union

1. The Government of Canada hereby recognises the presence on its territory of the already established Delegation of the European Union in Canada.

2. The Delegation of the European Union also represents the European Atomic Energy Community.

3. The Delegation of the European Union may only open offices forming part of the Delegation in localities, other than those where the Delegation itself is already established, with the prior express consent of Canada.

#### **ARTICLE 3**

##### Legal Personality

On the territory of Canada, the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community shall have full legal personality and the capacity to conclude contracts, to acquire and dispose of immovable and movable property, as necessary for the effective fulfilment of their duties, in accordance with Canadian law, and to conduct legal proceedings, and shall be represented for that purpose by the Head of Delegation.

#### **ARTICLE 4**

##### Privileges and Immunities

1. The Delegation of the European Union, its premises, property and archives shall, on the territory of Canada, enjoy such rights, privileges and immunities and be subject to such conditions and obligations as those accorded to diplomatic missions established in Canada under the Convention.

2. The Head of Delegation and the Delegation’s diplomatic staff, as well as members of their families forming part of their respective households, shall, on the territory of Canada, enjoy such rights, privileges and immunities and be subject to such conditions and obligations as those accorded to diplomatic staff and members of their families forming part of their respective households under the Convention.

3. The other provisions of the Convention apply *mutatis mutandis*.

4. The rights, privileges and immunities provided for in this Article shall be accorded on condition that, in conformity with the provisions of Article 16 of *Protocol 7 on the Privileges and Immunities of the European Union* annexed to the *Treaty on the European Union and* the *Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community*, the Member States of the European Union accord the same rights and privileges and immunities to the Mission of Canada to the European Union, to its Head and to its diplomatic staff, as well as to the members of their families forming part of their respective households.

5. The rights, privileges and immunities referred to in paragraphs 1 to 3 of this Article shall not apply to any staff member of the Delegation of the European Union or member of their families forming part of their respective households if they are a Canadian citizen or a Permanent Resident of Canada.

#### **ARTICLE 5**

##### Laissez-Passer

The Government of Canada hereby recognises the *laissez-passer* issued by the European Union to officials and other servants of its institutions as valid travel documents.

#### **ARTICLE 6**

##### Settlement of Disputes

The Parties shall settle any dispute concerning the interpretation of this Agreement by consultations with the aim of arriving at conciliation.

#### **ARTICLE 7**

##### Amendments

The Parties may amend this Agreement by mutual agreement. Any amendment enters into force in accordance with the procedures set out in Article 9.

#### **ARTICLE 8**

##### Termination of the Agreement

A Party wishing to denounce this Agreement shall give written notice to the other Party through diplomatic channels. This Agreement terminates one year after the date on which the written notice was received by the other Party, or on any other date as decided by the Parties.

#### **ARTICLE 9**

##### Entry into Force

This Agreement enters into force on the date of the notification from Canada to the European Union and the European Atomic Energy Community informing of the completion of Canada’s domestic procedures necessary for the entry into force of this Agreement.
