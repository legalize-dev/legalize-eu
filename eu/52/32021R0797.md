# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/797 of 8 March 2021 correcting certain language versions of Annex II and Annex VI to Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures (Text with EEA relevance)

> **This is the law as enacted. Amendments are not incorporated below — each one is
> a separate file in this repository and a commit in this file's history.**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19.5.2021 | EN | Official Journal of the European Union | L 176/1 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2008 on classification, labelling and packaging of substances and mixtures, amending and repealing Directives 67/548/EEC and 1999/45/EC, and amending Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 [^ntr1-L_2021176EN.01000101-E0001], and in particular Article 53(1) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) The Danish, French, German, Italian and Polish language versions of Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 contain an error in the introductory sentence of the second paragraph of section 2.12 of Part 2 of Annex II as regards the scope of the labelling obligation for solid mixtures containing titanium dioxide.

- (2) The Czech language version of Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 contains additional errors in section 2.12 of Part 2 of Annex II as regards the labelling obligation for mixtures containing titanium dioxide.

- (3) The Italian language version of Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 contains an error in note 10 of section 1.1.3.2 of Part 1 of Annex VI as regards the hazard classification of mixtures containing titanium dioxide.

- (4) The Czech, Danish, French, German, Italian and Polish language versions of Annex II and the Italian language version of Annex VI to Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 should therefore be corrected accordingly. The other language versions are not affected,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

*(does not concern the English version)*

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 8 March 2021.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
