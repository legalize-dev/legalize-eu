# Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/2362 of 26 October 2026 amending Decision (CFSP) 2021/509 establishing a European Peace Facility

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Articles 28(1), 41(2), 42(4) and 30(1) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, with the support of the Commission,

Whereas:

- (1) Council Decision (CFSP) 2024/890 [^ntr1-L_202602362EN.000101-E0001], amending Decision (CFSP) 2021/509 [^ntr2-L_202602362EN.000101-E0002] establishing a European Peace Facility (EPF), increased the financial ceiling of the EPF by an amount of EUR 5 000 million (in current prices) dedicated to continuing to address the needs of the Armed Forces of Ukraine through support for the provision of both lethal and non-lethal military support and training (the so-called ‘Ukraine Assistance Fund’, UAF). The annual ceilings for commitments established in Annex I of Decision (CFSP) 2021/509 were adapted by Decision (CFSP) 2024/890 to allow for a mobilisation of the UAF in three years, from 2025 to 2027.

- (2) In view of the political agreement reached by the Political and Security Committee (PSC) on 25 September 2026 on the mobilisation of the EPF funds available for Ukraine, including under the UAF, it is necessary to revise the distribution of the annual ceilings for commitments of Decision (CFSP) 2021/509.

- (3) Decision (CFSP) 2021/509 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

Decision (CFSP) 2021/509 is amended as follows:

- (1) In Article 2, a title is inserted, to read as follows:

> ‘Article 2

> Financial ceiling’

;

- (2) Annex I is replaced by the following:

> ‘ANNEX I

> ANNUAL FINANCIAL CEILINGS

> The annual appropriations shall be authorised within the limits of the following amounts, without prejudice to Article 17(3) and (3a), and subject to Article 73(2) and (10):

> Current prices, in million euro

> |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> |  | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 |
> | Current prices | 399 | 591 | 971 | 2 082 | 1 644 | 5 079 | 6 274 ’. |

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 26 October 2026.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**M. HEYDON**
