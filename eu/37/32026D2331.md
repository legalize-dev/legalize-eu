# Political and Security Committee Decision (CFSP) 2026/2331 of 13 October 2026 on the appointment of the Head of the Civilian Pillar of the European Union Support Mission in Lebanon (EUSM Lebanon) (EUSM Lebanon/1/2026)

#### Article 1

Mr Robert Edwin BOER is appointed as Head of the Civilian Pillar of the European Union Support Mission in Lebanon (EUSM Lebanon) from 1 November 2026 to 31 October 2027.

#### Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

It shall apply from 13 October 2026.
