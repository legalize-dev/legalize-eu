THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU) 2024/386 of 19 January 2024 establishing restrictive measures against those who support, facilitate or enable violent actions by Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad [^ntr1-L_202401838EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 13(1) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

- (1) On 19 January 2024, the Council adopted Regulation (EU) 2024/386.

- (2) In view of the continued threat posed by Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad, six persons and three entities should be added to the lists in Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/386.

- (3) Regulation (EU) 2024/386 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/386 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 28 June 2024.

***For the Council***

***The President***

**H. LAHBIB**
