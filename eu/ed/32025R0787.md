THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 concerning the placing of plant protection products on the market and repealing Council Directives 79/117/EEC and 91/414/EEC [^ntr1-L_202500787EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 17, first paragraph, thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 192/2014 [^ntr2-L_202500787EN.000101-E0002] approved the active substance 1,4-dimethylnaphthalene until 30 June 2024.

- (2) Commission Directive 2008/40/EC [^ntr3-L_202500787EN.000101-E0003] included amidosulfuron as an active substance in Annex I to Council Directive 91/414/EEC [^ntr4-L_202500787EN.000101-E0004] until 31 December 2018.

- (3) Commission Directive 2000/68/EC [^ntr5-L_202500787EN.000101-E0005] included bentazone as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 July 2011 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/660 [^ntr6-L_202500787EN.000101-E0006] renewed the approval of that active substance until 31 May 2025.

- (4) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 350/2013 [^ntr7-L_202500787EN.000101-E0007] approved the active substance bixafen until 30 September 2023.

- (5) Commission Directive 2007/76/EC [^ntr8-L_202500787EN.000101-E0008] included clomazone and fludioxonil as active substances in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 October 2018.

- (6) Commission Directive 2008/66/EC [^ntr9-L_202500787EN.000101-E0009] included fenoxaprop-P as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 December 2018.

- (7) Commission Directive 2008/44/EC [^ntr10-L_202500787EN.000101-E0010] included fluoxastrobin as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 July 2018.

- (8) Commission Directive 2008/108/EC [^ntr11-L_202500787EN.000101-E0011] included flutolanil as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 28 February 2019.

- (9) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 192/2014 [^ntr12-L_202500787EN.000101-E0012] approved the active substance 1,4-dimethylnaphthalene until 30 June 2024.

- (10) Commission Directive 2008/127/EC [^ntr13-L_202500787EN.000101-E0013] included gibberellic acid and gibberellins as active substances in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 August 2019.

- (11) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 589/2012 [^ntr14-L_202500787EN.000101-E0014] approved the active substance fluxapyroxad until 31 December 2022.

- (12) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1165 [^ntr15-L_202500787EN.000101-E0015] approved the active substance halauxifen-methyl until 5 August 2025.

- (13) Commission Directive 2003/70/EC [^ntr16-L_202500787EN.000101-E0016] included mecoprop-P as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 May 2014.

- (14) Council Directive 2009/117/EC [^ntr17-L_202500787EN.000101-E0017] included paraffin oil as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 December 2019.

- (15) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1187/2013 [^ntr18-L_202500787EN.000101-E0018] approved the active substance penthiopyrad until 30 April 2024.

- (16) Commission Directive 2007/52/EC [^ntr19-L_202500787EN.000101-E0019] included pirimiphos-methyl as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 30 September 2017.

- (17) Commission Directive 2007/25/EC [^ntr20-L_202500787EN.000101-E0020] included propamocarb as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 30 September 2017.

- (18) Commission Directive 2003/39/EC [^ntr21-L_202500787EN.000101-E0021] included propyzamide as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 March 2014 and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/755 [^ntr22-L_202500787EN.000101-E0022] renewed the approval of that active substance until 30 June 2025.

- (19) Commission Directive 2008/44/EC [^ntr23-L_202500787EN.000101-E0023] included prothioconazole as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 July 2018.

- (20) Commission Directive 2006/39/EC [^ntr24-L_202500787EN.000101-E0024] included rimsulfuron as an active substance in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC until 31 January 2017.

- (21) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 826/2013 [^ntr25-L_202500787EN.000101-E0025] approved the active substance sedaxane until 31 January 2024.

- (22) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1295 [^ntr26-L_202500787EN.000101-E0026] approved the active substance sulfoxaflor until 18 August 2025.

- (23) According to Article 78(3) of Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009, active substances included in Annex I to Directive 91/414/EEC are deemed to have been approved under Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009.

- (24) The active substances amidosulfuron, clomazone, fenoxaprop-P, fludioxonil, fluoxastrobin, flutolanil, gibberellic acid, gibberellins, mecoprop-P, paraffin oil, pirimiphos-methyl, propamocarb, prothioconazole and rimsulfuron were included in Part A of the Annex to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 540/2011 [^ntr27-L_202500787EN.000101-E0027]. The active substances 1,4-dimethylnaphthalene, bentazone, bixafen, fluxapyroxad, halauxifen-methyl, penthiopyrad, sedaxane and sulfoxaflor were included in Part B, and the active substance propyzamide was included in Part E of the Annex to that Regulation.

- (25) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/2007 [^ntr28-L_202500787EN.000101-E0028] extended the approval period of the active substance 1,4-dimethylnaphthalene until 30 June 2025, and the approval periods of the active substances bixafen, fluxapyroxad, penthiopyrad and sedaxane until 31 May 2025.

- (26) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/2592 [^ntr29-L_202500787EN.000101-E0029] extended the approval periods of the active substances amidosulfuron, fenoxaprop-P and paraffin oil until 15 August 2025.

- (27) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1757 [^ntr30-L_202500787EN.000101-E0030] extended the approval periods of the active substances clomazone and fludioxonil until 15 June 2025.

- (28) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/918 [^ntr31-L_202500787EN.000101-E0031] extended the approval periods of the active substances fluoxastrobin, pirimiphos-methyl and propamocarb until 15 June 2025, and the approval period of the active substance prothioconazole until 15 August 2025.
