# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2538 of 17 November 2026 correcting Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1919 imposing a definitive anti-dumping duty and definitively collecting the provisional duty imposed on imports of certain hot-rolled flat products of iron, non-alloy or other alloy steel originating in Egypt, Japan and Vietnam, and terminating the investigation on imports thereof originating in India

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2016/1036 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2016 on protection against dumped imports from countries not members of the European Union [^ntr1-L_202602538EN.000101-E0001] (‘the basic Regulation’), and in particular Articles 9 and 14(1) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) By Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1919 [^ntr2-L_202602538EN.000101-E0002] (‘the original Regulation’), the European Commission (‘the Commission’) imposed definitive anti-dumping duties on imports of certain hot-rolled flat products of iron, non-alloy or other alloy steel originating in Egypt, Japan and Vietnam.

- (2) In the investigation leading to the imposition of definitive anti-dumping duties, sampling was used when investigating exporting producers, in accordance with Article 17 of the basic Regulation.

- (3) In September 2026, Tokyo Steel Manufacturing Co., Ltd., a sampled Japanese exporting producer whose products were made subject to the individual anti-dumping duty of 6,9 % contacted the Commission, bringing to its attention an error in the way the name of its company appeared in the original Regulation, namely as Tokyo Steel Co. Ltd.. A new analysis of the documents already submitted by the company during the original investigation, among which their Articles of Incorporation, confirmed that the party’s name was misspelled in the original Regulation.

- (4) In view of the above, it is considered necessary to amend the original Regulation from the day of its entry into force to reflect the correct name of the relevant exporting producer, which is Tokyo Steel Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

- (5) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 15(1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/1036,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Article 1(2) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1919 is amended as follows:

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ‘Japan | Tokyo Steel Co. Ltd. | 6,9 % | 89MA’ |

is replaced by:

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ‘Japan | Tokyo Steel Manufacturing Co., Ltd. | 6,9 % | 89MA’ |

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

It shall apply from 27 September 2025.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 17 November 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
