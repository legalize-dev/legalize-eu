# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2199 of 2 October 2026 cancelling the Union authorisation for the single biocidal product Nordkalk QL 0-2 in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1481

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products [^ntr1-L_202602199EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 49 thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) On 29 May 2024, Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1481 [^ntr2-L_202602199EN.000101-E0002] granted a Union authorisation with the authorisation number EU-0029370-0000 to the authorisation holder Nordkalk AB for the making available on the market and use of the same single biocidal product ‘Nordkalk QL 0-2’ in accordance with Article 44(5), first subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012.

- (2) On 21 May 2026, Nordkalk AB submitted to the European Chemicals Agency (‘the Agency’) a request to cancel that Union authorisation in accordance with Article 49 of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012. The request was recorded under case number BC-NU118405-18 in the Register for Biocidal Products.

- (3) On 21 May 2026, Nordkalk AB had submitted a reasoning for the request for cancellation, providing that it had decided to discontinue the production of ‘Nordkalk QL 0-2’ based on commercial considerations.

- (4) On 22 May 2026, the Agency forwarded the reasoned request to the Commission.

- (5) It is therefore appropriate to cancel the Union authorisation for the single biocidal product ‘Nordkalk QL 0-2’ at the reasoned request of the authorisation holder and consequently to repeal Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1481.

- (6) It is also appropriate to grant a period of grace for the making available on the market and the use of the existing stocks in accordance with Article 52 of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The Union authorisation for the single biocidal product ‘Nordkalk QL 0-2’ granted by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1481 to Nordkalk AB with the authorisation number EU-0029370-0000 is cancelled.

#### Article 2

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/1481 is repealed.

#### Article 3

The biocidal product ‘Nordkalk QL 0-2’ shall no longer be made available on the market from 25 April 2027 and existing stocks of such biocidal product shall no longer be used from 25 October 2027.

#### Article 4

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 2 October 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
