THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on European standardisation, amending Council Directives 89/686/EEC and 93/15/EEC and Directives 94/9/EC, 94/25/EC, 95/16/EC, 97/23/EC, 98/34/EC, 2004/22/EC, 2007/23/EC, 2009/23/EC and 2009/105/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council and repealing Council Decision 87/95/EEC and Decision No 1673/2006/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr1-L_202602505EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 10(6) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1971 [^ntr2-L_202602505EN.000101-E0002] amends Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1279 [^ntr3-L_202602505EN.000101-E0003] in order to publish in the *Official Journal of the European Union* the references of four harmonised standards and withdraws the references of seven harmonised standards, drafted in support of Regulation (EU) 2016/425 of the European Parliament and of the Council [^ntr4-L_202602505EN.000101-E0004].

- (2) It is necessary to correct Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1971 in order to rectify an error in Article 2 as regards the date of application of point (1) of Annex I of that Decision. That point should apply 18 months from the date of publication of that Decision.

- (3) Since Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1971 applies from 4 September 2026, the correction provided for in this Decision should also apply from that date in order to ensure consistency and legal certainty.

- (4) In view of the need to rectify that error without delay, this Decision should enter into force on the day of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

#### Article 1

##### Correction to Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1971

In Article 2 of Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1971, the following second paragraph is added:

> ‘Point (1) of Annex I shall apply from 4 March 2028.’

#### Article 2

##### Entry into force

This Decision shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

It shall apply from 4 September 2026.

Done at Brussels, 12 November 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
