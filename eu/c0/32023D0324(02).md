# Council Decision of 21 March 2023 appointing an alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for Denmark 2023/C 109/03

#### Article 1

The following person is hereby appointed alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the period ending on 28 February 2025:

##### II.   **TRADE UNION REPRESENTATIVES**

| **Member State** | **Alternate member** |
| --- | --- |
| Denmark | Mr Ulrik SPANNOW |

#### Article 2

The Council shall appoint the members and alternate members not yet nominated at a later date.

#### Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.
