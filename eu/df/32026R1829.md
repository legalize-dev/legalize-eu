# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1829 of 24 July 2026 amending Annexes II and III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards specific hygiene rules for live bivalve molluscs

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Image omitted; see official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32026R1829) | Official Journal of the European Union | EN L series |

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | 2026/1829 | 30.10.2026 |

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 29 April 2004 laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin [^ntr1-L_202601829EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 10(1), second subparagraph, points (a) and (e), thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 lays down specific rules on the hygiene of food of animal origin for food business operators. In particular, Article 5(1), point (b), of that Regulation requires food business operators not to place on the market a product of animal origin, including live bivalve molluscs, unless it has an identification mark, to be applied in accordance with Section I of Annex II to that Regulation.

- (2) In addition, according to Section VII, Chapter I, points 9(b) and (c), of Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004, intermediary operators can send live bivalve molluscs from class B or C production areas to purification centres or to processing establishments respectively. Since these molluscs are not fit for direct human consumption but are nevertheless to bear an identification mark as they are placed on the market, it is appropriate to clarify that they should not bear an identification mark. Section I, Part A, point 3, of Annex II to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 should be amended accordingly.

- (3) Section VII of Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 refers to re-immersion of live bivalve molluscs either in a natural site or in an establishment. However, Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 does not define the activity of re-immersion, and this can create ambiguity. That activity should therefore be defined. Section VII of Annex III to that Regulation should be amended accordingly.

- (4) Section VII, Chapter I of Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 lays down the general requirements for the placing on the market of live bivalve molluscs, including, in point 4, the information that the registration document which accompanies a batch of live bivalve molluscs is to contain. In order to reduce administrative burden for operators and competent authorities performing official controls, food business operators should be required to provide the registration document in accordance with a model available in the Trade Control and Expert System (TRACES). To comply with the standardised model available in TRACES, Section VII, Chapter I, of Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 should therefore be amended accordingly. To reflect the amendment of the information to be included in the registration document, Section VII, Chapter X, of Annex III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004, which lays down the model registration document accompanying a batch of live bivalve molluscs, should also be amended.

- (5) In order to reduce administrative burden for operators and competent authorities performing official controls, food business operators should also be required to issue in TRACES the registration document that is to accompany a consignment of live bivalve molluscs. Considering the complexity of the practical arrangements for food business operators to issue the registration document in TRACES, it is appropriate to provide for sufficient time to allow competent authorities and operators to comply with the obligation to use TRACES,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

Annexes II and III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 are amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 24 July 2026.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**

## ANNEX

Annexes II and III to Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 are amended as follows:

- (1) in Annex II, Section I, point A.3 is replaced by the following:

> - ‘3. An identification mark is not required:

> - — on packs of eggs when a packing centre code is applied in accordance with Part A of Annex XIV to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council[^ntr*1-L_202601829EN.000301-E0001],

> - — in the case of live bivalve molluscs sent unpurified from an approved intermediary operator to another operator irrespective of the classification of the production area.
