# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/2893 of 15 December 2023 entering a name in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications [Φακή Εγκλουβής / Faki Eglouvis (PDO)]

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 November 2012 on quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs [^ntr1-L_202302893EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 52(2) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Pursuant to Article 50(2)(a) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012, Greece’s application to register the name ‘Φακή Εγκλουβής / Faki Eglouvis’ was published in the *Official Journal of the European Union* [^ntr2-L_202302893EN.000101-E0002].

- (2) As no statement of opposition under Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 has been received by the Commission, the name ‘Φακή Εγκλουβής / Faki Eglouvis’ should therefore be entered in the register,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

The name ‘Φακή Εγκλουβής / Faki Eglouvis’ (PDO) is hereby entered in the register.

The name specified in the first paragraph denotes a product in Class 1.6. Fruit, vegetables and cereals, fresh or processed, as listed in Annex XI to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 668/2014 [^ntr3-L_202302893EN.000101-E0003].

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 15 December 2023.

***For the Commission,***

***On behalf of the President,***

**Janusz WOJCIECHOWSKI**

***Member of the Commission***
