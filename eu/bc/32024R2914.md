THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2018/1091 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 July 2018 on integrated farm statistics and repealing Regulations (EC) No 1166/2008 and (EU) No 1337/2011 [^ntr1-L_202402914EN.000101-E0001], and in particular Article 5(4) and Article 8(1) thereof,

Whereas:

- (1) Regulation (EU) 2018/1091 provides for both a framework for European statistics on agricultural holdings and for the combination of information on their structure with information on production methods, rural development measures, agro-environmental aspects and other related information.

- (2) For the reference year 2026, and pursuant to Article 5(4) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1091, the Commission should adopt an implementing act specifying the description of variables listed in Annex III to the Regulation referring to the core structural data variables.

- (3) Pursuant to Article 8(1) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1091, the Commission is to list and describe the variables to be collected for the reference year 2026 corresponding to the topics and detailed topics in the following modules of Annex IV to the Regulation: ‘Labour force and other gainful activities’, ‘Rural development’, ‘Animal housing and manure management’ and ‘Vineyards’.

- (4) The measures in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the European Statistical System Committee [^ntr2-L_202402914EN.000101-E0002].

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

#### Article 1

1. The description of the core structural data variables listed in Annex III to Regulation (EU) 2018/1091 is set out in Annex I to this Regulation.

2. The list of the variables for topics and detailed topics in each module is set out in Annex II.

3. The descriptions of the variables to be used by the Member States for the topics and detailed topics in each module as listed in Annex II are set out in Annex III.

#### Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the *Official Journal of the European Union*.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 25 November 2024.

***For the Commission***

***The President***

**Ursula VON DER LEYEN**
